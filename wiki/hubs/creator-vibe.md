# Creator vibe

## Orientation

This Hub synthesizes **Power to Creators → Vibe**. “Vibe” is not used as a synonym for superficial style. It names contextual appraisal—what fits, feels credible, carries status, or deserves attention—when formal production becomes easier. The collection’s central question is what human capability remains worth cultivating when machines can generate plausible artifacts on demand.

## Synthesis

The collection advances a recurring sequence: production cost falls; output expands; selection and trust become harder; differentiation shifts toward judgment, taste, context, and responsibility. [[abundance-response-stack|The abundance response stack]] makes this sequence actionable without assuming every scarce layer is beneficial or fairly allocated.

The strongest tension is between **access** and **cultural weight**. Generative tools let nonprofessionals externalize imagination, but feed incentives and low-cost imitation can produce disposable output and culturally mark the result as ersatz. The Debate keeps both mechanisms visible.

## Emerging propositions

- Cheap production may increase the status value of demonstrable human responsibility, distinctive judgment, and work that resists standardized patterns.
- Deliberately slow, subtractive interfaces may improve judgment under abundance, but their benefits and accessibility remain untested.

Both are tracked in the synthesis backlog.
