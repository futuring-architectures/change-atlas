# Networks

## Orientation

This Hub synthesizes the curated **After the Internet → Networks** collection. The collection treats a network as more than connected nodes. Its outcomes depend on who can connect, what travels, how attention is allocated, which actions generate value, who captures that value, and where decisions are made.

Three maps recur across the Evidence. First, explicit social connection is being displaced in some products by platform inference and recommendation. Second, denser connection increases diffusion while also increasing homogenization, contagion, and dependence on a small set of viable designs. Third, “decentralization” is not a single quantity: technical distribution, local decision agency, ownership, exit, and public benefit can move independently.

The 52 entries span 1993–2024, but chronology alone does not establish a Trend. Most excerpts are interpretations, design claims, or predictions. The three Signals below are bounded observations; candidate directional propositions remain in the synthesis backlog until later collections supply more independent cases and temporal coverage.

## Emerging propositions not yet promoted

- Recommendation may be replacing explicit subscription as the dominant allocation mechanism on large content platforms, but the current cases are concentrated in 2022–2023.
- AI-driven content abundance may concentrate attention on already legible creators rather than democratize reach.
- Media may increasingly become a feature bundled into broader non-media offerings.
- Employee-built internal followings may rewire organizational authority independently of formal hierarchy.
- Ownership may help protocols become communities, but financial participation may also reproduce speculation, influencer culture, and concentration.
- A new internet wave may arise from cultural exit from current digital systems, but the present support is explicitly predictive.

These propositions have stable identities in `system/synthesis-backlog.json`; later relevant collections must record whether they strengthen, qualify, contest, promote, retire, or leave them unchanged.

## Synthesis boundary

This map derives from 52 curated entries and 103 atomic claims. It distinguishes observations from mechanisms, positions, and forecasts. The collection supports reusable Concepts, Frameworks, and Debates; it does not yet support a corpus-level claim that decentralization, recommendation, bundling, or cultural islanding is moving consistently in one direction.
