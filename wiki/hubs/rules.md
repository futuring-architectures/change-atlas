## Orientation

This Hub compiles the first synthesis of the curated **After the Internet → Rules** collection. The territory is not regulation alone: it includes the categories used to understand technological systems, where power enters their design and operation, and whether familiar oversight mechanisms still work.

The current Evidence is strongest on concepts, frameworks, and disagreements. It contains only a few narrowly scoped observations, so no Trend has yet been promoted.
