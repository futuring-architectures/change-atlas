# Sustaining

## Orientation

This Hub synthesizes **How We Learn → Sustaining**. The collection asks what survives after the breakthrough, product launch, automation push, or heroic intervention. Its answer is not stasis. Sustaining is the capacity to generate variation, select responsibly, diffuse benefits, preserve context, develop people, and renew the system before its current curve expires.

## Synthesis

### A capability is only durable if it can be renewed

Breakthrough narratives foreground named inventors and products. The Sustaining collection foregrounds the unnamed production improvements, hands-on access, communities, career paths, recognition, capital recycling, and late-entry routes that keep a capability alive. [[capability-renewal-infrastructure|Capability-renewal infrastructure]] distinguishes present output from the system's ability to produce its next generation of practitioners and improvements.

### Innovation includes diffusion and maintenance

An organization can build ideas while managing innovation investment badly. A society can chase frontier breakthroughs while failing to deploy proven achievements. [[innovation-ecology|Innovation ecology]] therefore includes variation, selection, diffusion, reinvestment, context preservation, and renewal. The [[sustaining-capacity-loop|Sustaining capacity loop]] turns these into an audit.

### Assistance can consume the capacity it appears to augment

AI-generated output raises the question of whether people still practice persistence, verification, and participation. [[capacity-erosion-through-assistance|Capacity erosion through assistance]] does not treat reduced effort as automatic decline; prompting can be managerial work. The discriminating evidence is longitudinal: can people still detect error, transfer knowledge, challenge the system, and act when the assistant changes or disappears?

### Context becomes infrastructure at machine speed

Generated volume makes complete line-by-line comprehension implausible, but abandoning understanding creates [[cognitive-debt|Cognitive debt]]. The sustainable alternative is not documentation volume. It is owned, tested, recoverable context: design systems, provenance, assumptions, interfaces, and feedback that people and tools can carry forward without propagating an inaccurate reference at machine speed.

### Betterment must be separated from throughput

[[type-two-growth|Type 2 growth]] names qualitative improvement that need not track material expansion. It is a useful possibility, not a resolved empirical Trend. Intangible systems retain material footprints, rebound can erase efficiency, and aggregate betterment can coexist with unequal access. The claim becomes useful only when its outcomes and resource boundary are specified.

## Emerging propositions

- Organizations that measure renewal capacity alongside present throughput may preserve capability through technological transitions better than organizations optimized only for current output.
- AI assistance that removes practice without preserving challenge, verification, and participation may create delayed capacity loss invisible in short-term productivity metrics.
- Machine-speed production may make owned and testable context a more important organizational asset than documentation volume.

These remain synthesis-backlog propositions for the Transitions collection and future evidence.
