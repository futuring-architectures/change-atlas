# Governance

## Orientation

This Hub synthesizes the curated **Nature of the Firm → Governance** collection. Its central concern is not governance as formal compliance. It is how firms choose an organizing logic, distribute authority, see what is happening inside themselves, and remain accountable for technologies whose scale, abuse potential, and failure patterns exceed familiar categories.

The collection contains strong Concepts and one applicable Framework, but few dated observations of named cases. No Trend is promoted, and an apparent efficiency–resilience tension remains an open research problem rather than a balanced Debate.
