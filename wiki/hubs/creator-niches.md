# Creator niches

## Orientation

This Hub synthesizes **Power to Creators → Niches**. Niches can multiply the number of viable “bests” by matching differentiated work with differentiated taste. Yet discoverability is not livelihood: creators still need financial runway, portable relationships, bargaining power, and protection from platform dependence.

## Synthesis

The collection separates three layers often collapsed into “the creator economy”: **allocation** (who is discoverable), **infrastructure** (who can start and operate), and **livelihood** (who can persist). Higher-resolution taste can diversify allocation; zero-to-one tools can widen entry; neither guarantees stable income or independence.

It also shows that creators do not simply replace institutions. Employers can become audience-building partners, and platforms can turn apparent independence into continuous self-marketing and dependency. [[practical-creator-arc|The Practical creator arc]] treats runway and protected attention as infrastructure rather than romanticizing the leap.

## Emerging propositions

- Plural taste systems may create more category leaders without flattening power-law inequality within each niche.
- Agent-mediated software discovery may improve the economics of occasional-use niche tools while concentrating control in the agent layer.

Both remain in the synthesis backlog until later collections provide adoption and outcome evidence.
