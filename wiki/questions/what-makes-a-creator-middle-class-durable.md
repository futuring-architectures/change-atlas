# What makes a creator middle class durable?

## Why it matters

Niche discovery can create more visible creators without producing stable livelihoods. A durable middle class requires persistence, mobility, and resilience across platform, market, and life changes.

## What is currently known

- [[creators-built-direct-audience-careers-outside-algorithmic-feeds|Selected direct-audience careers]] demonstrate possibility but not distribution.
- [[high-resolution-taste-markets|High-resolution taste markets]] diversify allocation.
- [[practical-creator-arc|The Practical creator arc]] identifies runway and protected attention.
- [[creator-as-platform-dependent-entrepreneur|Creator as platform-dependent entrepreneur]] identifies structural dependence.

## Missing evidence

- Income distributions, survival rates, volatility, benefits, debt, and unpaid work across creator cohorts.
- Audience and payment portability after platform or employer exit.
- Mobility from entry to stable livelihood rather than temporary participation.
- Comparative outcomes across subscription, patronage, employment, ownership, and agent-mediated discovery models.

## Resolution

_Open. The corpus establishes mechanisms and exemplars, not a durable class._
