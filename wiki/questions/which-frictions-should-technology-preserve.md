# Which frictions should technology preserve?

## Why it matters

Convenience and efficiency are powerful design objectives, but removing resistance can also remove rehearsal, feedback, reflection, memory, appeal, and the chance to notice error. “Friction” is too coarse a category for deciding what to automate or accelerate.

## What is currently known

- [[productive-friction|Productive friction]] names resistance that contributes to a valued capability rather than merely consuming resources.
- [[ai-tutors-can-t-solve-bloom-s-two-sigma-problem-evi-b70a2a6f8279#Claim 1|AI Tutors Can't Solve Bloom's Two Sigma Problem — claim 1]] argues that learning requires energy, struggle, and failure.
- [[overpromising-and-stumbling-bambis-evi-e3bf5de3c032#Claim 1|Overpromising & Stumbling Bambis — claim 1]] treats some early imperfection as a condition of experimentation.
- [[1979-evi-d9574be8c003#Claim 1|1979 — claim 1]] shows that maintaining old systems can preserve expertise on which newer services still depend.
- [[does-progress-require-acceleration-or-productive-friction|The acceleration-versus-friction Debate]] shows that the same delay can appear as obstruction or protection from different positions.

## Missing or discriminating evidence

- Comparative learning, safety, agency, and inclusion outcomes before and after a friction is removed.
- Evidence that a difficulty generates transferable capability rather than avoidable burden.
- Distributional analysis of who performs the effort and who receives the benefit.
- Signals that patience is producing learning rather than protecting a failing system.
- Designs that preserve feedback and recourse while removing administrative and accessibility barriers.

## Suggested next sources or actions

- Classify frictions by function: rehearsal, reflection, verification, coordination, recourse, exclusion, or waste.
- Test the smallest removable unit instead of treating an entire workflow as frictionless or friction-heavy.
- Track delayed effects such as skill retention, dependency, correction quality, and maintenance capacity.

## Resolution

_Open. The collection establishes several functions of friction but not a general rule for deciding which instances to preserve._
