# How can participation scale without exhausting creators?

## Why it matters

Low-friction participation expands access and contribution, but each interaction can generate review, support, moderation, and emotional labor. A community can grow its output and simultaneously destroy the capacity that sustains it.

## What is currently known

- [[creator-attention-is-a-depletable-commons|Creator attention is a depletable commons]] identifies the constraint.
- [[community-sustainability-audit|The Community sustainability audit]] makes attention, rights, rewards, care, and exit inspectable.
- [[my-boundaries-as-an-open-source-developer-evi-07ff0d7ceb6e#Claim 1|My boundaries — claim 1]] supplies an individual enforcement mechanism.

## Missing evidence

- Comparative maintainer outcomes under funding, delegation, moderation, documentation, and contribution-gating models.
- Measures of response burden, burnout, newcomer development, project quality, and contributor diversity.
- Cases where growth supports a larger maintainer core rather than concentrating demand.

## Resolution

_Open. The collection identifies the inverted commons but not a tested scaling model._
