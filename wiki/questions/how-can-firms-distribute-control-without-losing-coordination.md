# How can firms distribute control without losing coordination?

## Why it matters

Ecosystem advantage depends on actors outside central command, yet products and services still need compatibility, priorities, safety boundaries, dispute handling, and investment. Centralization can suppress local knowledge and stakeholder agency; unstructured distribution can fragment responsibility and make collective action impossible.

## What is currently known

- [[ecosystem-advantage|Ecosystem advantage]] shifts capability from ownership toward access and relationships.
- [[repeating-the-mistake-of-abstracting-social-connections-in-system-design-evi-b08090ed1e8e#Claim 1|Repeating the Mistake — claim 1]] proposes fractal scaling from local teams; [[repeating-the-mistake-of-abstracting-social-connections-in-system-design-evi-b08090ed1e8e#Claim 2|claim 2]] notes that local tools can reduce dependence on centralized IT deployment.
- [[squad-wealth-evi-dbf9042a8b6e#Claim 1|Squad Wealth — claim 1]] makes collective self-recognition a prerequisite for coherent informal groups.
- [[open-source-ai-is-the-path-forward-evi-1eb62fe74eb7#Claim 1|Open Source AI — claim 1]] shows a sponsor seeking distributed complement production around a common platform.
- [[fantasy-miniatures-run-on-a-modular-global-supply-chain|The fantasy-miniature supply chain]] shows coordination through marketplaces, licenses, subscriptions, payments, standards, and specialist firms rather than one hierarchy.

## Missing or discriminating evidence

- Comparative failure and recovery across centralized, federated, market, cooperative, and protocol coordination.
- Which decisions can remain local and which require common standards or accountable escalation.
- How sponsor control changes after complementors make irreversible investments.
- Measures of responsiveness, coherence, duplicated effort, bargaining power, and participant recourse.
- Cases where distributed control improved outcomes without making accountability diffuse.

## Suggested next sources or actions

- Trace one decision across local team, orchestrator, infrastructure provider, and affected participant.
- Record who can change standards and who bears migration cost.
- Test the system under disagreement and withdrawal, not only normal collaboration.

## Resolution

_Open. The collection supplies coordination mechanisms and normative positions, but no comparative architecture for distributing control._
