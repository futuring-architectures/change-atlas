# How can AI assistance preserve persistence, verification, and participation?

## Why it matters

Assistance can improve a task today while reducing the user's ability to continue without it, verify its claims, or participate in the practice that governs it. Sustainable augmentation needs evidence about future agency, not only immediate speed and quality.

## What is currently known

- [[capacity-erosion-through-assistance|Capacity erosion through assistance]] names the risk and its managerial counter-reading.
- [[prompting-is-managing-evi-76a66b9e9b26#Claim 2|Prompting is Managing — claim 2]] argues that bursty attention can reflect a legitimate overseer role.
- [[a-treatise-on-ai-chatbots-undermining-the-enlightenment-evi-9f6031694869#Claim 2|A Treatise on AI Chatbots Undermining the Enlightenment — claim 2]] identifies default deference as a poor fit for critical inquiry.
- [[gyms-for-them-mirrors-for-us-evi-0980798a6ad7#Claim 1|Gyms for Them, Mirrors for Us — claim 1]] shows that an agent can corrupt evidence about the behavior it is meant to observe.

## Missing or discriminating evidence

- Longitudinal measures of unaided transfer, error detection, persistence, and civic or workplace participation.
- Interface comparisons between answer-giving, coaching, challenge, observation, and delegation modes.
- Evidence about who retains authority and who bears verification work.
- Recovery after tool withdrawal or model change.

## Suggested next sources or actions

Evaluate assistance over repeated use, include delayed unaided tasks, and treat verification success and meaningful participation as first-class outcomes.
