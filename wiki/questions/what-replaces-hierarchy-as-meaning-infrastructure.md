# What replaces hierarchy as meaning infrastructure?

## Why it matters

Flattening an organization can remove bureaucracy and shorten information paths. It can also remove places where people learn judgment, interpret ambiguous priorities, build identity, and locate responsibility. If AI replaces routing without replacing those functions, the result may be informationally efficient but developmentally thin.

## What is currently known

- [[hierarchy-as-meaning-infrastructure|Hierarchy as meaning infrastructure]] distinguishes information movement from interpretation and development.
- [[the-edge-assumes-a-self-evi-f23289a89b75#Claim 2|The Edge Assumes a Self — claim 2]] argues that the second is not automatically solved by automating the first.
- [[the-age-of-de-bossification-evi-a96cd272949d#Claim 2|The Age of De-Bossification — claim 2]] proposes leadership through influence, coaching, and culture rather than monitoring.
- [[the-cognition-stack-for-ai-native-companies-evi-137042c99711#Claim 2|The Cognition Stack — claim 2]] retains human taste and final selection in an AI-native organization.

## Missing or discriminating evidence

- Longitudinal cases of flatter organizations that develop novice judgment without recreating informal hierarchy.
- Comparative evidence for peer mentoring, craft communities, apprenticeship, rotating stewardship, rituals, and AI-supported coaching.
- Measures of shared context, role identity, ethical responsibility, learning, and decision quality—not only speed or span of control.
- Failure cases where removing managers also removed escalation, care, or accountable interpretation.

## Suggested next sources or actions

- Trace how a newcomer learns “what good looks like” in an AI-heavy team.
- Map who interprets conflict between local objectives and organizational purpose.
- Compare formal hierarchy with peer, community, and protocol-based meaning mechanisms.
