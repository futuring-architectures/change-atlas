# How is judgment cultivated when production is cheap?

## Why it matters

Judgment develops through making, feedback, failure, apprenticeship, and responsibility. If production is delegated before those experiences occur, people may retain authority without capacity.

## What is currently known

- [[responsibility-gap-after-automation|The responsibility gap]] separates nominal approval from meaningful ownership.
- [[abundance-response-stack|The Abundance response stack]] identifies formulation, verification, selection, trust, slowness, subtraction, and responsibility.
- [[writes-and-write-nots-evi-bbb3e7a0bd86#Claim 2|Writes and Write-Nots — claim 2]] treats practice as thinking.
- [[how-can-ai-mediated-work-preserve-apprenticeship-and-meaning|The apprenticeship Question]] links judgment to social infrastructure.

## Missing evidence

- Longitudinal skill development in AI-assisted and manually intensive practice.
- Methods that retain productive struggle without preserving pointless friction.
- Measures of transfer, error recognition, ethical reasoning, taste, and responsibility.
- Designs matching accountability with authority and learning opportunity.

## Resolution

_Open. The corpus names the capabilities and risks but supplies no evaluated learning architecture._
