# When should resistance change or stop a technology transition?

## Why it matters

Calling resistance ignorance can suppress evidence about harm and legitimacy. Treating every objection as a veto can also prevent learning and leave system design to less accountable actors. Transitions need a fair way to interpret resistance and connect it to decisions.

## What is currently known

- [[the-new-luddites-arent-backing-down-evi-8b5c99287abc#Claim 1|The New Luddites Aren't Backing Down — claim 1]] reports objections that survive factual correction; [[the-new-luddites-arent-backing-down-evi-8b5c99287abc#Claim 2|claim 2]] interprets them as a commonality conflict.
- [[dispatch-from-the-trenches-of-the-butlerian-jihad-evi-5b801932134f#Claim 2|Dispatch from the Trenches — claim 2]] argues that disengagement can surrender shaping power.
- [[red-lining-ai-evi-946f46f44c16#Claim 1|Red-lining AI — claim 1]] supplies grounds for opposing exploitative uses; [[red-lining-ai-evi-946f46f44c16#Claim 2|claim 2]] supports selective boundaries rather than categorical bans.
- [[banning-ai-in-law-school-weve-seen-this-before-evi-207599420750#Claim 2|Banning AI in Law School — claim 2]] argues that expressed concern is not demonstrated harm; [[banning-ai-in-law-school-weve-seen-this-before-evi-207599420750#Claim 6|claim 6]] proposes enforcing harm-specific rules rather than banning a technical category.

## Missing or discriminating evidence

- Cases where resistance changed design, ownership, or deployment and altered outcomes.
- Procedures that distinguish factual, distributive, procedural, and moral objections.
- Evidence thresholds that connect an objection to a plausible harm mechanism without requiring irreversible harm to occur first.
- Evidence about whose refusal is honored and whose is externalized.
- Conditions under which experiments are genuinely voluntary and reversible.

## Suggested next sources or actions

- Compare contested transitions with and without formal stop and redesign rights.
- Record objections as testable claims and value conflicts rather than one sentiment score.
- Track whether participation redistributes decision power or merely legitimizes a fixed plan.

## Resolution

_Open. The corpus supports selective, reasoned opposition and now makes the burden of proof more explicit, but it does not settle how much evidence is enough when harms may be high-stakes, involuntary, or irreversible._
