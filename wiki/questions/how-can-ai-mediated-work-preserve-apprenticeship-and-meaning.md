# How can AI-mediated work preserve apprenticeship and meaning?

## Why it matters

AI can make expert help and production cheaper while removing interactions through which novices learn context, colleagues recognize contribution, and teams construct shared purpose. A workflow can improve task throughput while degrading the capability that sustains it.

## What is currently known

- [[the-programmer-identity-crisis-evi-122ddfa90b55#Claim 2|The Programmer Identity Crisis — claim 2]] identifies avoidance of human contact as a plausible default.
- [[setting-time-on-fire-and-the-temptation-of-the-button-evi-e24400dc15b6#Claim 2|Setting time on fire — claim 2]] identifies lost meaning in machine-to-machine work chains.
- [[hierarchy-as-meaning-infrastructure|Hierarchy as meaning infrastructure]] shows that information access and developmental interpretation are distinct functions.
- [[collaboration-architecture-choice|Collaboration architecture choice]] makes social infrastructure an explicit design input.
- [[hardcore-ai-for-history-evi-235c91d99d6a#Claim 1|Hardcore AI for History — claim 1]] forecasts model output becoming a new performance floor.
- [[when-we-become-cogs-evi-c1da269b7217#Claim 1|When We Become Cogs — claim 1]] identifies loss of full-problem understanding under easier workflows.
- [[brain-drain-evi-21d6d2449459#Claim 1|brAIn drAIn — claim 1]] predicts different cognitive effects for skilled and dependent users.

## Missing or discriminating evidence

- Longitudinal comparisons of novice development in AI-heavy and peer-heavy workflows.
- Measures of tacit knowledge, escalation judgment, belonging, recognition, and retention alongside output.
- Practices that use AI to augment rather than replace mentoring and peer review.
- Failure cases where short-run individual productivity damaged system capability.

## Suggested next sources or actions

- Trace how a novice learns exceptions and standards in an AI-mediated team.
- Compare protected apprenticeship time with purely on-demand machine assistance.
- Audit which interactions disappeared after AI adoption and which functions they previously carried.

## Resolution

_Open. The collection supplies plausible mechanisms but no evaluated organizational design._
