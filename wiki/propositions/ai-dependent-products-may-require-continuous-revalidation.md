# AI-dependent products may require continuous revalidation

## Proposition

AI-dependent products may require continuous revalidation as upstream model behavior, capability, and cost change.

**Scope:** Products and services with material dependencies on externally evolving AI models.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **After The Internet Paradigms:** The collection identifies upstream model change and integration churn, but does not yet supply a longitudinal product case or reusable operating model.
- **Nature Of The Firm Organization:** The AI-native organization source supplies a reusable operating requirement: data, evaluation, governance, learning loops, monitoring, and correction. It strengthens continuous revalidation as a practice, though it still lacks a longitudinal upstream-model-change case.
- **How We Learn Architectures:** Generative-AI red teaming as part of product construction, stochastic-software ambiguity, and changing agent interfaces strengthen the case for continuous revalidation beyond conventional release testing.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- **Nature Of The Firm Ecosystems (`no_change`):** The Llama source makes an AI platform dependent on a changing complement ecosystem, but provides no product revalidation process or longitudinal case of upstream model change.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
