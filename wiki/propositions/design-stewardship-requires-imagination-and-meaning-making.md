# Design stewardship requires imagination and meaning-making

## Proposition

Design stewardship may require imagination and meaning-making beyond the instrumental logic of design as a business tool.

**Scope:** Design practice, organizational culture, and technological transition.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **Nature Of The Firm Governance:** The proposition is normatively clear but lacks an operational account of stewardship or evidence from a design practice.
- **Nature Of The Firm Culture:** The designer blind-spots source adds a concrete design-practice rationale: designers can expose inherited frames and unserved demographics. It strengthens the imagination claim but still lacks participatory authorship and evaluated stewardship outcomes.
- **Power To Creators Vibe:** Vibe adds art as technological foresight, problem formulation, curiosity, taste, and responsibility as non-instrumental design capabilities. It strengthens the rationale but still lacks evaluated participatory practice.
- **How We Learn Futuring:** Product vision, intuition beyond existing data, design for breakage, inclusive scenario language, and shared AI surfaces strengthen imagination and meaning-making as stewardship capacities. Evaluated stewardship practice remains absent.
- **How We Learn Architectures:** Design for emergence, difficult simplicity, narrative world-building, and human-centered interface constraints strengthen the operational account of imaginative stewardship. Comparative stewardship outcomes remain absent.
- **How We Learn Sustaining:** Scenario experimentation, systematic thinking, deliberate horizon expansion, and critical rather than deferential AI strengthen imagination and meaning-making as design capacities. Evaluated stewardship outcomes remain absent.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- The evidence remains heterogeneous and does not establish a universal or deterministic effect.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
