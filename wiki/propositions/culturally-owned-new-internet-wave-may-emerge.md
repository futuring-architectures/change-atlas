# A culturally owned new internet wave may emerge

## Proposition

Demand for culturally owned digital spaces may support a future internet wave among younger participants, but prior reform movements have fragmented and no younger-led migration is observed at scale.

**Scope:** Consumer social internet spaces and younger digital participants.

**Status:** `qualified`

## Why it may hold

- **After The Internet Networks:** The proposition is explicitly conditional and predictive; later batches should look for observed cultural exit, new interaction models, and disconfirming continuity.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- **After The Internet Paradigms (`contested`):** The collection documents a broad New Internet reform movement but reports that it fractured, lost momentum, and failed to dislodge incumbents. This contests near-term emergence without ruling out a later generation or different architecture.
- **After The Internet Culture (`qualified`):** Manifestos for a more beautiful or shared private internet show persistent demand for culturally owned spaces, but the collection does not observe a new wave or establish that the demand is specifically younger-led.
- **How We Learn Futuring (`qualified`):** Young people integrating virtual and physical worlds, diminished agency onramps, and calls for culturally owned information ecologies support the motivation for a new wave. The collection does not observe younger participants building or migrating to one at scale.
- **How We Learn Transitions (`qualified`):** Luddite and AI-resistance accounts strengthen the motive to seek culturally ownable alternatives, while also showing that refusal can target uses and institutions rather than produce a new internet wave.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
