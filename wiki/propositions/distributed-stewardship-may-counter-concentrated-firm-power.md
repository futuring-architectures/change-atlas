# Distributed stewardship may counter concentrated firm power

## Proposition

Distributed stewardship may counteract the concentration of decision power in conventional firms.

**Scope:** Organizational governance and firm boundaries.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **Nature Of The Firm Governance:** Governance evidence supplies a plausible contrast between distributed stewardship and upward-concentrating firm structure, but no observed organizational case.
- **Nature Of The Firm Ecosystems:** The Ecosystems collection adds a stakeholder mechanism: permeable firms can depend on orbital contributors who lack formal access, voice, or ownership, while community-powered digital firms rarely use cooperative structures. It strengthens the stewardship case but still lacks a comparative distributed-governance outcome.
- **Nature Of The Firm Organization:** The Organization collection adds de-bossification as an explicit alternative to monitoring and control and an AI-native requirement for earned trust before flattening. This strengthens the design rationale but still supplies no observed comparative stewardship outcome.
- **Power To Creators Communities:** Communities adds active citizenship, local agency, maintainer boundaries, and direct-audience relationships as mechanisms for distributed action. It strengthens the stewardship rationale but still lacks a comparative transfer of formal decision power.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- **Nature Of The Firm Culture (`no_change`):** Culture supplies indirect influence, autonomy, dissent, and narrative mechanisms but no case in which distributed stewardship changes formal decision power or produces comparative outcomes.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
