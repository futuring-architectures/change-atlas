# AI substitution for colleagues may erode apprenticeship and meaning

## Proposition

AI-mediated substitution for colleagues may erode apprenticeship, recognition, and shared meaning even when individual task output improves.

**Scope:** Collaborative knowledge work and workplace learning.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **Nature Of The Firm Culture:** Two sources identify lost peer contact, developmental interaction, recognition, and meaning as plausible consequences of AI-mediated work. They provide mechanisms and forecasts, not observed longitudinal outcomes.
- **Power To Creators Gameplay:** The collection identifies crutch effects, loss of full-problem understanding, role narrowing, and disappearing entry-level practice. It strengthens the mechanism without longitudinal apprenticeship or belonging outcomes.
- **How We Learn Sustaining:** Capacity erosion through assistance, missing practice infrastructure, and critical-chatbot design strengthen the apprenticeship and meaning-loss mechanism. Longitudinal workplace evidence remains absent.
- **How We Learn Transitions:** Epistemic fluency, social disagreement, and task-boundary work strengthen the mechanism by which private AI substitution can remove relational learning and recognition; longitudinal apprenticeship outcomes remain absent.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- **How We Learn Architectures (`qualified`):** Extended-mind theory and learning-style evidence show that AI-mediated performance can either extend cognition or remove needed structure. The collection sharpens boundary conditions but does not observe apprenticeship or meaning loss longitudinally.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
