# AI content abundance may concentrate attention on legible creators

## Proposition

AI-driven content abundance may concentrate attention on already legible creators, although niche discovery and new creator infrastructure may produce countervailing gains.

**Scope:** Digital creator markets and algorithmically mediated audience discovery.

**Status:** `qualified`

## Why it may hold

- **After The Internet Networks:** The mechanism is coherent but the concentration outcome is predictive; later creator and culture collections should test discovery, fandom, and new-entry cases.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- **After The Internet Paradigms (`qualified`):** Machine-scale production strengthens the abundance and selection-pressure mechanism, but this collection does not observe whether attention accrues to incumbent creators, new entrants, or fragmented niches.
- **After The Internet Culture (`qualified`):** Culture evidence strengthens the mechanism: selection can reward transmissibility, legibility, and provenance. It still does not show that incumbent creators rather than new entrants or niches capture the resulting attention.
- **Power To Creators Niches (`qualified`):** High-resolution taste and zero-to-one creator infrastructure provide counter-mechanisms through which abundance could support new niche leaders. The collection still lacks comparative attention distributions for incumbents and entrants.
- **Power To Creators Vibe (`qualified`):** Mass nonprofessional image generation strengthens abundance, while status legitimacy, taste, and low-prestige AI aesthetics show competing selection mechanisms. No attention distribution resolves who benefits.
- **Power To Creators Gameplay (`qualified`):** Gameplay adds long-tail aggregator capture, recreated NFT gatekeeping, and cheap-content quality risks, while citizen development and user programming provide counter-mechanisms. It still supplies no comparative attention distribution between incumbents, entrants, and niches.
- **How We Learn Futuring (`qualified`):** Algorithmic monoculture and synthetic information trash strengthen abundance and legibility mechanisms, while signal rewilding and relational access offer counter-mechanisms. No comparative attention distribution identifies who gains reach.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
