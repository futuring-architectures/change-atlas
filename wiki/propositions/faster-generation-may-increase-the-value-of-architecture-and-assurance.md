# Faster generation may increase the value of architecture and assurance

## Proposition

Faster component generation may increase the relative value of architecture, assurance, and institutional absorption rather than eliminate them.

**Scope:** Software-intensive organizations using generative AI for implementation.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **How We Learn Architectures:** Architecture, assurance, and institutional-clock mechanisms converge, but the collection does not measure their relative value as generation accelerates.
- **How We Learn Sustaining:** Cognitive debt, verification erosion, and machine-speed propagation from inaccurate design systems strengthen architecture, assurance, and institutional absorption as post-generation constraints.
- **How We Learn Transitions:** Cheap implementation, collapsing artifact signals, task-boundary work, pace layering, and outcome drift strengthen the case that evaluation, architecture, and institutional absorption become relatively scarcer.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- The evidence remains heterogeneous and does not establish a universal or deterministic effect.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
