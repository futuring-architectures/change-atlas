# Enterprise AI optimization may shift to useful intelligence per dollar

## Proposition

Enterprise AI optimization may shift from maximizing model capability toward maximizing useful intelligence per dollar.

**Scope:** Enterprise selection and deployment of AI models and applications.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **After The Internet Paradigms:** The proposition is timely and measurable but currently rests on one reported enterprise interpretation and a forecast rather than comparative deployment data.
- **Nature Of The Firm Organization:** The MIT computer-vision study shows that technical task exposure can remain economically unattractive after deployment costs are modeled. This strengthens the value-per-dollar mechanism without directly comparing enterprise model choices.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- **Nature Of The Firm Ecosystems (`no_change`):** The collection contains ecosystem and distribution arguments but no comparative enterprise evidence about useful intelligence per dollar.
- **How We Learn Architectures (`no_change`):** The collection discusses duplicated compute investment and falling software-production cost but adds no comparative useful-intelligence-per-dollar measure or optimization case.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
