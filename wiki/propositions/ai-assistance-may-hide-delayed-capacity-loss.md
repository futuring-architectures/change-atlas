# AI assistance may hide delayed capacity loss

## Proposition

AI assistance that removes practice without preserving challenge, verification, and participation may create delayed capacity loss invisible in short-term productivity metrics.

**Scope:** AI-assisted learning and knowledge work measured primarily through near-term output.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **How We Learn Sustaining:** The collection supplies capacity-loss mechanisms and a managerial-attention counter-reading but no longitudinal productivity-and-capacity outcome.
- **How We Learn Transitions:** Epistemic fluency, disagreement, and task-boundary work specify capacities that assistance can bypass or preserve. The delayed-loss claim still lacks longitudinal measurement.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- The evidence remains heterogeneous and does not establish a universal or deterministic effect.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
