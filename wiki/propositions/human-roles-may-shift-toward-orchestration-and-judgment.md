# Human roles may shift toward orchestration and judgment

## Proposition

Human organizational roles may shift from executing bounded processes toward orchestration, evaluation, judgment, and exception handling.

**Scope:** Knowledge work in organizations adopting AI and programmable workflows.

**Status:** `strengthened`

## Why it may hold

- **Nature Of The Firm Organization:** Several independent authors forecast orchestration and judgment roles as process execution becomes cheaper, while the workslop case shows evaluation and repair work already moving across workflows. The corpus lacks representative role-transition and employment evidence.
- **Nature Of The Firm Culture:** The Culture collection independently argues that cheap generation shifts scarcity toward collective commitment and forecasts substitution of machine assistance for peer collaboration. It strengthens the role-shift mechanism but remains forecast-heavy and lacks representative job-transition evidence.
- **Power To Creators Gameplay:** Multiple sources move human work toward intent, architecture, curation, verification, integration, and exception handling. Most are practitioner interpretations or forecasts, so prevalence and distributional outcomes remain open.
- **How We Learn Architectures:** Software oversight, programmable-organization roles, capability allocation, AI auditors, and ideation bottlenecks all move human work toward orchestration, judgment, evaluation, and exceptions. Most claims remain forecasts rather than role-level outcome studies.
- **How We Learn Sustaining:** Prompting as management, human-token complementarity, and design-system ownership further shift roles toward oversight, judgment, context, and exception handling. Role-level outcomes remain predictive.

## Qualifications and counter-evidence

- The evidence remains heterogeneous and does not establish a universal or deterministic effect.

## What would change the status

- **Strengthen:** add independent observations that show the proposed mechanism and outcome within the stated scope.
- **Qualify:** identify repeatable boundary conditions or countervailing mechanisms and revise the statement to express them.
- **Contest:** add credible evidence that the predicted direction, mechanism, or scope is materially wrong.
- **Promote to Trend:** establish a dated directional pattern across independent observations, with explicit scope and period.
