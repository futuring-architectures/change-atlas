# Quibi bypassed emerging social-video talent

## Observation

A 2020 Bloomberg postmortem reported that Quibi relied on expensive established producers and stars while doing little to recruit emerging talent already successful on YouTube.

## Why it matters

The case shows an incumbent production logic overlooking talent validated by a networked creator ecology. It does not establish that this choice caused Quibi's failure; product design, timing, distribution, and leadership also mattered.
