# Corner stores adopt digital intermediaries

## Observation

Louise Matsakis reported in 2021 that millions of mom-and-pop shops were already using technology supplied by retail start-ups.

## Why it matters

The case shows digitization occurring through coexistence between independent firms and technology intermediaries, not only through replacement by a vertically integrated platform. It does not yet show durable merchant autonomy, fair terms, or improved outcomes.
