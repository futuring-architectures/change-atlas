# Early GPT-4 fell behind small open models

## Observation

A late-2024 practitioner account noted that GPT-4-0613 had fallen to 65th on LM Arena behind small open models. Saturated evaluations and stable chat interfaces made the accumulated improvement less visible to ordinary users.

## Why it matters

Perceived and measured progress can diverge. The example does not settle whether frontier scaling itself was slowing or whether leaderboard gains translated into useful systems.
