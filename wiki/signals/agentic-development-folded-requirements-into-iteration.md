# Agentic development folded requirements into iteration

## Observation

A February 2026 account of agentic software development describes a workflow in which a human supplies direction, an agent produces a feature version, and the human evaluates and redirects. Multiple cheap versions make requirements an artifact of iteration rather than a completed phase before implementation.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: one practitioner account of AI-assisted development.
- Observation date: 2026-02-20.
- Date confidence: publication date verified; prevalence is not established.

## Evidence

- [[the-software-development-lifecycle-is-dead-evi-de2981d8e0b9#Claim 1|The Software Development Lifecycle Is Dead — claim 1]] describes the phase shift.
- [[the-software-development-lifecycle-is-dead-evi-de2981d8e0b9#Claim 2|The Software Development Lifecycle Is Dead — claim 2]] describes generation-and-selection as the alternative loop.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- Regulated, safety-critical, and highly coupled systems may retain extensive up-front requirements.
- Cheap generation does not establish cheap evaluation, integration, or failure.
- The source is a practitioner interpretation, not representative adoption data.
