# AI-assisted building lacks stable craft vocabulary

## Observation

Scott Werner’s 2025 account describes AI-assisted building as a practice without settled vocabulary or durable expertise because tools change faster than conventions accumulate.

## Why it matters

Naming difficulty is evidence of an unsettled craft boundary, not proof that technical skill has vanished. The account is situated and requires broader practice evidence.
