# Citizen development moved employees from users to builders

## Observation

A 2023 Harvard Business Review account described citizen development changing employees from relatively powerless software users into builders who could improve or automate their own work.

## Why it matters

The case is a concrete precursor to natural-language software creation. It shows agency moving toward domain workers, while leaving open whether organizations later recentralize control through platforms, permissions, security rules, and maintenance obligations.
