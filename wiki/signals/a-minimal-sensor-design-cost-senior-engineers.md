# A minimal sensor design cost senior engineers

## Observation

Asad Madni instructed engineers to include only what a life-saving sensor absolutely needed. Some experienced engineers resisted what they perceived as simple work, and the project lost senior designers. Madni's interpretation was that achieving the required simplicity was harder than producing a more complex design.
