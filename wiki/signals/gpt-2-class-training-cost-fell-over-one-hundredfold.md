# GPT-2-class training cost fell over one hundredfold

## Observation

Jack Clark estimated in 2024 that compute and algorithmic improvement had reduced GPT-2-class training cost to roughly $250—more than a hundredfold decline over five years.

## Why it matters

Capability control becomes harder when yesterday’s frontier becomes cheap and reproducible. The figure is approximate and excludes data, labor, energy, expertise, and deployment.
