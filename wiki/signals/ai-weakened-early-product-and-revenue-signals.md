# AI weakened early product and revenue signals

## Observation

In August 2025, seed investor Sam Lessin argued that a plausible app no longer demonstrated the same founder understanding or design judgment because AI could generate it cheaply. He also treated initial revenue as less discriminating when APIs and liquid global markets made a few early sales easier.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: seed-stage software investment and product evaluation.
- Observation date: 2025-08-06.
- Date confidence: post date verified; the signal degradation is one investor's interpretation.

## Evidence

- [[ai-destroys-seed-investors-ability-to-analyze-product-evi-3a9c8c5b925a#Claim 1|AI destroys seed investors' ability to analyze product — claim 1]] addresses product artifacts.
- [[ai-destroys-seed-investors-ability-to-analyze-product-evi-3a9c8c5b925a#Claim 2|AI destroys seed investors' ability to analyze product — claim 2]] addresses early revenue.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- Retention, unit economics, user learning, and operating judgment may remain strong signals.
- Cheaper prototypes can reveal founder learning faster when evaluation is redesigned.
- The observation does not establish investor behavior or startup outcomes across a sample.
