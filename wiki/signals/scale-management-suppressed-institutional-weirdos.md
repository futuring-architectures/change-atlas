# Scale management suppressed institutional weirdos

## Observation

A June 2022 analysis of prestigious institutions describes an emergent transition: rules, policies, and layers accumulate to make scale manageable, and the resulting environment suppresses unconventional thinkers without any individual deciding to eliminate creativity.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: large prestigious organizations.
- Observation date: 2022-06-09.
- Date confidence: publication date verified; the organizational pattern is an author synthesis.

## Evidence

- [[too-big-to-think-why-prestigious-institutions-stopped-generating-good-ideas-evi-39a9227c4064#Claim 1|Too Big To Think — claim 1]] identifies scale-management structure.
- [[too-big-to-think-why-prestigious-institutions-stopped-generating-good-ideas-evi-39a9227c4064#Claim 2|Too Big To Think — claim 2]] identifies the unintended creativity effect.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- Rules can protect fairness, safety, memory, and coordination.
- Some large institutions deliberately preserve protected variation.
- The source does not compare creativity outcomes across institution sizes.
