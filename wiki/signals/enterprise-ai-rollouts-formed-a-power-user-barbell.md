# Enterprise AI rollouts formed a power-user barbell

## Observation

An August 2026 account by an enterprise-agent services founder reports a recurring usage distribution after organizational AI rollout: about 5–10% power users, about 20% weak users, and about 70% who barely use the tool. In one operations organization, dashboard adoption rose but team speed did not; in another unnamed enterprise commitment, roughly 10% of users consumed 90% of tokens.

The account distinguishes access and login activity from competent use. It describes proficient use as context management, reusable instructions, selective routing between model judgment and deterministic code, and inspection before acceptance.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: founder-reported enterprise implementations and executive conversations across organizations described as ranging from about 50 to 5,000 people.
- Observation date: published 2026-08-07; individual rollout dates are not supplied.
- Date confidence: publication date verified from the rendered source; case timing and measurements are unresolved.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- The cases are unnamed, self-reported, and supplied by a founder whose company sells the recommended implementation approach.
- User categories, token consumption, and team speed do not establish quality, profitability, safety, worker agency, or causal effects of the rollout.
- A barbell may reflect product design, task fit, incentives, workflow contradictions, unequal access to practice, or managerial choices rather than fixed individual aptitude.
- The source cites external adoption and EBIT figures, but this Signal does not independently verify or rely on them.
