# Radiologist work grew alongside better models

## Observation

A 2025 account found that diagnostic models raised productivity without reducing radiologist work. Diverse tasks, high stakes, elastic demand, behavior, institutions, and incentives mediated deployment.

## Why it matters

Task capability does not mechanically determine employment. The case is domain-specific and does not guarantee similar complementarity elsewhere.
