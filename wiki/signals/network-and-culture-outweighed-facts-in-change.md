# Network and culture outweighed facts in change

## Observation

An April 2026 account of social change argues that the beliefs and behavior of surrounding people are stronger predictors of action than information supplied to an isolated individual. Durable change therefore requires work on networks and culture in addition to facts and slogans.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: organizational and social behavior change.
- Observation date: 2026-04-12.
- Date confidence: publication date verified; “outweighed” is a synthesis claim, not a reported effect size.

## Evidence

- [[stop-trying-to-educate-people-into-changing-science-proves-it-doesnt-work-evi-733a74727c05#Claim 1|Stop Trying to Educate People Into Changing — claim 1]] states the social-influence mechanism.
- [[stop-trying-to-educate-people-into-changing-science-proves-it-doesnt-work-evi-733a74727c05#Claim 2|Stop Trying to Educate People Into Changing — claim 2]] draws the network-and-culture implication.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- Education can be essential when combined with incentives, networks, and structural change.
- Networks can reinforce misinformation and exclusion.
- The excerpt does not compare intervention outcomes.
