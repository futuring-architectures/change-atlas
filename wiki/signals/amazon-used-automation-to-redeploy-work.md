# Amazon used automation to redeploy work

## Observation

Alex Kantrowitz reported in 2020 that Amazon's automation initiative was intended to remove tasks and reassign people to building new products, not to do the same work with fewer people.

## Why it matters

The case separates technical automation from the organizational choice that follows it. Redeployment is an observable alternative to immediate labor extraction, but the stated purpose does not establish firm-wide employment effects or how gains were distributed.

- [[how-amazon-automated-work-and-put-its-people-to-better-use-evi-897e78794bed#Claim 1|How Amazon Automated Work — claim 1]] reports the initiative and its stated staffing purpose.
