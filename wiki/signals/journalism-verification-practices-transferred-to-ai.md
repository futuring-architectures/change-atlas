# Journalism verification practices transferred to AI

## Observation

A 2025 account of ProPublica’s investigative practice argued that journalists were unusually well prepared to use language models because they already derive truth from multiple unreliable sources and routinely fact-check claims.

## Why it matters

The case suggests that adoption depends on inherited verification institutions, not only model capability. It does not show that fact-checking alone resolves confidentiality, provenance, copyright, or representational harms.
