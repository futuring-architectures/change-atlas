# Vertical AI teams hired domain context after technical founding

## Observation

A 2025 investor account described many vertical-AI companies as founded by people with deep model expertise but limited industry context, which they then hired early.

## Why it matters

The sequence separates technical differentiation from contextual defensibility. The account is selected rather than representative and does not yet show whether hired context becomes authority or remains advisory.
