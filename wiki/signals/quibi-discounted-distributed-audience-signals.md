# Quibi discounted distributed audience signals

## Observation

Salil Dalvi's retrospective account describes Jeffrey Katzenberg's personal taste as Quibi's dominant quality signal and contrasts it with billions of views directed toward creators on YouTube, Instagram, and Snapchat. The organization had access to a wider cultural field but did not treat its signals as authoritative.

## Why it matters

External access is not ecosystem learning. Firms need mechanisms that translate distributed behavior into decisions and allow those signals to challenge internal status and intuition. Otherwise “ecosystem strategy” can mean extracting demand while preserving a closed decision process.

- [[learning-from-quibi-evi-3c7738eb54ad#Claim 1|Learning From Quibi — claim 1]] supplies the leadership-judgment interpretation.

## Limits

- The original article has migrated and its date is recovered only to month precision.
- The excerpt does not document Quibi's complete research, commissioning, product, or launch process.
- Views on free creator platforms are not direct evidence of willingness to pay for Quibi.
