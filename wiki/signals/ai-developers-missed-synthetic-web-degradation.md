# AI developers missed synthetic web degradation

## Observation

A 2024 critique attributed proliferating synthetic clickbait and information trash partly to generative-AI deployment and argued that developers had not anticipated this downstream effect.

## Why it matters

The case motivates designing for breakage even when a precise failure cannot be predicted. Platform incentives, publishers, and users share causality, so it is not evidence of a single-actor failure.
