# An agent workflow displaces human review and planning

## Observation

In the engineering practice described by John Allsopp, an AI-agent workflow omits human code review, iterates against a test suite, and selects the next ticket without explicit waterfall or agile planning routines.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: The particular workflow described by the source; not software engineering or AI-assisted development generally.
- Observation date: Reported on 2026-03-02; the underlying practice is not separately dated.
- Date confidence: High for publication, low for the start and duration of the observed practice.

## Evidence

- [[the-structure-of-engineering-revolutions-evi-0c30a7f66a56#Claim 1|Engineering Revolutions — claim 1]] reports the omission of human review and substitution of test iteration.
- [[the-structure-of-engineering-revolutions-evi-0c30a7f66a56#Claim 3|Engineering Revolutions — claim 3]] reports the replacement of explicit planning routines with agent ticket selection.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- The source does not identify prevalence, organization, codebase, or measured outcomes.
- Strategic planning or human review may occur outside the workflow boundary described in the excerpt.
- The source's contrast may foreground a novel extreme rather than a representative practice.
