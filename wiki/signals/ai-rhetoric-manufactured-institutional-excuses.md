# AI rhetoric manufactured institutional excuses

## Observation

A 2025 analysis described “AI can do it” as a justification for removing labor forces, regulatory capacity, or accountability, shifting debate from whether removal was desirable to how machine replacement would work.

## Why it matters

Forecasts do political work in the present. Capability claims can allocate permission before capability or legitimacy is established.
