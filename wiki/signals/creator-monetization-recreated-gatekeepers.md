# Creator monetization recreated gatekeepers

## Observation

Chris Stokel-Walker’s 2022 account reported that many NFT projects valued roadmaps and institutional promises more than the attached art. The institutions forming around those projects performed familiar gatekeeping and value-capture functions.

## Why it matters

Changing the technical ownership mechanism does not automatically remove intermediation. A new layer can reproduce the old power relation under a decentralization narrative.
