# AI-generated prose weakened word-count proxies

## Observation

Ethan Mollick’s 2024 account identified words in emails, reports, and presentations as common proxies for effort, intelligence, and care. Generative tools could satisfy those visible conventions rapidly without demonstrating equivalent reasoning.

## Why it matters

Cheap artifacts make artifact-based supervision less informative. Organizations must inspect claims, decisions, provenance, and outcomes rather than infer work quality from polish or volume.
