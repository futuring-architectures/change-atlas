# Meeting cancellation required an async replacement system

## Observation

A January 2023 async-work account distinguishes subtraction from transformation. Removing meetings at scale can erase a visible cost without replacing the context, decisions, coordination, and social habits that meetings carried.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: organizations moving toward async-first work.
- Observation date: 2023-01-18.
- Date confidence: publication date verified; the predicted regression is not a comparative outcome.

## Evidence

- [[you-can-t-just-cancel-76-500-hours-of-meetings-evi-f5b0086d68a1#Claim 1|You can't just cancel 76,500 hours of meetings — claim 1]] identifies the regression risk.
- [[you-can-t-just-cancel-76-500-hours-of-meetings-evi-f5b0086d68a1#Claim 2|You can't just cancel 76,500 hours of meetings — claim 2]] identifies replacement culture, systems, and habits.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- The source advocates async work and may understate cases where meeting removal alone eliminates low-value activity.
- Replacement systems can create documentation and notification overload.
- Different dependencies require different coordination forms.
