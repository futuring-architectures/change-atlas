# Creators built direct-audience careers outside algorithmic feeds

## Observation

Cal Newport documented selected creators who built successful careers without waiting for a Web3 breakthrough, relying instead on audiences willing to leave algorithmic streams and engage directly.

## Why it matters

The cases show direct relationship as an organizational capability, not a particular technology. They are selected successes and do not establish the size or income stability of a creative middle class.
