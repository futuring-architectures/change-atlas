# AI applications lagged model capabilities

## Observation

Arvind Narayanan’s late-2024 assessment argued that real-world application development lagged model capability and that useful existing capabilities remained underused.

## Why it matters

Model benchmarks are not adoption outcomes. Interfaces, workflows, institutions, costs, trust, incentives, and demand mediate the conversion of capability into value.
