# Gig platforms enabled remote work while capturing worker value

## Observation

Lynda Gratton’s 2024 review of her earlier workplace forecasts found that platforms made highly skilled remote freelancing feasible. It also found that many workers encountered an unregulated, low-wage arrangement in which platforms captured a substantial share of the value created.

## Why it matters

Capability and distribution are different outcomes. A system can expand who can participate while worsening the terms on which many participants do so.
