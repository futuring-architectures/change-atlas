# Recommendation algorithms projected cultural pasts forward

## Observation

A November 2021 analysis of algorithmic culture argues that translating taste into behavioral data lets recommendation loops repeat past practice. The system can optimize engagement correctly while narrowing the future toward patterns already legible in its training and feedback.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: data-driven cultural recommendation and production.
- Observation date: 2021-11-22.
- Date confidence: publication date verified; cultural freezing is an interpretation rather than a measured platform outcome.

## Evidence

- [[yesterday-once-more-evi-05bf12374ad2#Claim 1|Yesterday Once More — claim 1]] describes culture reduced to behavioral data.
- [[yesterday-once-more-evi-05bf12374ad2#Claim 2|Yesterday Once More — claim 2]] describes the past-projection mechanism.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- Recommendation systems can introduce exploration and surface archival or minority work.
- User choice and producer strategy also shape the loop.
- Cultural similarity over time is not itself proof of algorithmic causation.

- Exemplifies the [[activity-alignment-gap|Activity-alignment gap]]: a correctly operating system can sustain the wrong cultural outcome.
