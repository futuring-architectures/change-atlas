# AuctionWeb users supply unpaid marketplace infrastructure

## Observation

Users of AuctionWeb, the early eBay marketplace, contributed ratings, practical advice, and shipping guidance without direct pay. The source reports that this participant-created community layer increased the marketplace’s usefulness and interprets community and market as mutually reinforcing sources of financial value.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: The early AuctionWeb/eBay marketplace.
- Observation date: Historical case reported in a source published 2022-06-16; the excerpt does not state the underlying years.
- Date confidence: Source publication date verified; event-level timing unresolved.

## Evidence

- [[wallets-and-eyeballs-how-ebay-turned-the-internet-into-a-marketplace-evi-ff62765a864a#Claim 2|Wallets and Eyeballs — claim 2]] records the participant contributions.
- [[wallets-and-eyeballs-how-ebay-turned-the-internet-into-a-marketplace-evi-ff62765a864a#Claim 1|Wallets and Eyeballs — claim 1]] interprets community as enhancing marketplace value.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- The excerpt does not quantify participation, unpaid labor, or incremental marketplace value.
- Community labor may create shared value while also externalizing costs onto users; this case does not establish how value was distributed.
- A historical marketplace case does not prove that the same mechanism works in social networks, protocols, or creator communities.
