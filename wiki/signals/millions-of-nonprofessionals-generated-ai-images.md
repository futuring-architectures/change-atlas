# Millions of nonprofessionals generated AI images

## Observation

Kevin Kelly reported in November 2022 that millions of mostly nonprofessional users were already generating novel-looking images with a new class of AI tools.

## Why it matters

The case demonstrates a sharp reduction in technical access barriers. It does not establish originality, authorship, consent, quality, durable practice, or income.
