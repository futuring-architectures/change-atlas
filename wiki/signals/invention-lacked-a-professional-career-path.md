# Invention lacked a professional career path

## Observation

A 2021 diagnosis of the American progress ecosystem argues that invention—distinguished from scientific research, corporate engineering, and entrepreneurship—had no straightforward entry path, employing institution, recognized community, or prestige ladder through which successful practitioners gained their next opportunity.

## Scope and timing

- Scope: professional infrastructure for invention, primarily in the United States.
- Observation date: 2021-04-29.
- Date confidence: publication date verified; the institutional absence is an author assessment.

## Evidence

- [[we-need-a-career-path-for-invention-evi-0d32e8977be4#Claim 1|We need a career path for invention — claim 1]] identifies the missing entry role and institution.
- [[we-need-a-career-path-for-invention-evi-0d32e8977be4#Claim 2|We need a career path for invention — claim 2]] identifies the missing community and prestige system.

## Limitations and alternative readings

- Adjacent research, engineering, entrepreneurship, and maker communities can support some inventors.
- The account does not compare national systems or technical fields.
- A formal career path could professionalize invention while narrowing unconventional entry.
