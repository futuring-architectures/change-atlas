# SEO optimization degraded search usefulness

## Observation

Amanda Chicago Lewis documented in 2023 that SEO practices had made many search results feel commercially promotional and indirectly responsive.

## Why it matters

The case is a pre-generative-AI warning: when an allocator rewards legible optimization, abundant production can target the allocator rather than the user’s need.
