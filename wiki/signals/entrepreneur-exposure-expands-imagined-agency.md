# Entrepreneur exposure expands imagined agency

## Observation

Matt Clancy's 2021 research review reports a measurable positive effect of entrepreneurial exposure for people who normally might not imagine starting a business.

## Why it matters

Community changes more than information flow: nearby examples can make a form of action cognitively available. The excerpt does not state effect size, duration, or boundary conditions.
