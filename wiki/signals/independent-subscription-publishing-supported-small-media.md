# Independent subscription publishing supported small media

## Observation

Mike Solana described subscription publishing and creator-monetization tools as visibly supporting individual and small-media publishing in February 2021.

## Why it matters

The observation establishes a viable channel, not a sector-wide migration or durable income distribution. It provides one case for direct niche publishing while leaving survival, reach, and incumbent advantage unresolved.
