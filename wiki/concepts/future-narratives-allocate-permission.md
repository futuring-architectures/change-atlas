# Future narratives allocate permission

## Definition

Future narratives allocate permission when a forecast changes what can be funded, deployed, removed, regulated, or imagined in the present. “Inevitable” futures make political choices appear technical.

## Significance

Futures work is intervention, not only description. Responsible practice must ask who benefits if a scenario is believed, which alternatives become unthinkable, and what evidence could revise the narrative.

## Boundary

Narrative effects do not make every forecast cynical or false. They require the consequences of belief to be audited alongside predictive accuracy.
