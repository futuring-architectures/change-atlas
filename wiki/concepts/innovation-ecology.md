# Innovation ecology

## Definition

An innovation ecology sustains novelty through a recurring system: generate meaningful variation, select under evidence and constraints, diffuse what works, recycle capital and experience, maintain operating knowledge, and renew the portfolio before the current growth curve expires.

## Provenance

The concept draws on evolutionary analogies in Modus and Harvard Business Review, portfolio investment in Schumpeterian Waste, the diffusion-versus-breakthrough balance, and Tom Blomfield's startup flywheel.

## Aliases and related terms

- Innovation ecosystem
- Evolutionary innovation system
- Innovation farm
- Portfolio ecology

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Ecology does not mean unguided chaos; organizations must deliberately create variation and govern selection.
- Selection criteria embed power and values.
- Diffusion and maintenance are innovation functions, not merely post-innovation execution.
- Heroic individuals can matter without being a renewable system.
