# Structural uncertainty

## Definition

Structural uncertainty changes what actors are competing for, against, or within. It differs from operational uncertainty, where the structure of the problem remains stable and action concerns execution inside it.

## Provenance

Sangeet Paul Choudary distinguishes changes to the competitive playing field from changes in operating conditions and argues that operational responses are inadequate when the problem itself has changed: [[chegg-chatgpt-and-the-changing-nature-of-change-evi-90ef6bd085b1#Claim 1|uncertainty that changes the playing field]] and [[chegg-chatgpt-and-the-changing-nature-of-change-evi-90ef6bd085b1#Claim 2|the limits of operational responses]].

## Aliases and related terms

- Structural change
- Problem redefinition
- [[governance-category-mismatch|Governance category mismatch]]

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- The Evidence does not provide a test for distinguishing structural uncertainty from unusually severe operational uncertainty.
- Structural and operational problems can coexist; diagnosing one does not eliminate the other.
- The concept identifies why familiar responses may fail but does not itself specify a structural response.
