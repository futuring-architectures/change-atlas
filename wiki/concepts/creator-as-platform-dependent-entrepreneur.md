# Creator as platform-dependent entrepreneur

## Definition

Creator as platform-dependent entrepreneur names the tension between apparent independence and infrastructural dependence. Individuals market a self, audience, and output as assets while platforms govern visibility, metrics, monetization, and portability.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Direct monetization can increase agency relative to employment or gatekeeping even when dependency persists.
- Creator is a heterogeneous identity, not a single labor relation.
- Audience ownership is often partial when discovery, payment, and identity remain intermediated.
- Continuous self-marketing can blur leisure, expression, labor, and investment.
