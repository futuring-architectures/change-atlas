# Database-reality inversion

## Definition

Database-reality inversion occurs when a representation stops fitting lived conditions and an institution changes people or practice to preserve the representation. AI can intensify the inversion because its operation depends on data that is standardized, available, and legible to machines.

## Provenance

Nilay Patel describes a recurring software pattern: when the database diverges from reality, organizations often tweak the world rather than the database. The concept names the power relation hidden inside that convenience.

## Aliases and related terms

- Making reality fit the database
- Data-model coercion
- Legibility inversion
- Administrative reality distortion

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Standardization is not inherently coercive; shared representations enable coordination and rights administration.
- Every model simplifies. Inversion begins when preserving the model overrides corrective evidence or affected people's agency.
- Updating a database can also be the right intervention when practice is unsafe or inconsistent.
- AI dependence increases the incentive for machine legibility but does not determine the governance choice.
