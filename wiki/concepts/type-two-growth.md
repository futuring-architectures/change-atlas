# Type 2 growth

## Definition

Type 2 growth is improvement in qualitative or intangible dimensions—freedom, wisdom, complexity, safety, quality, options, and capability—without requiring proportional expansion in material quantity.

## Provenance

Kevin Kelly contrasts “moreness” with betterment and argues that evolutionary growth can continue after material expansion reaches ecological or demographic limits.

## Aliases and related terms

- Betterment growth
- Qualitative growth
- Intangible growth
- Dematerialized progress

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Intangible improvement still depends on material and energy infrastructure.
- Betterment is difficult to measure and can conceal unequal distribution.
- Lower material intensity per unit does not guarantee lower total throughput.
- The claim that betterment is unlimited is a forecast, not an observed boundary.
