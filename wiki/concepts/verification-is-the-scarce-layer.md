# Verification is the scarce layer

## Definition

Verification is the scarce layer when producing a plausible artifact costs less than establishing whether it is true, appropriate, safe, original, complete, and owned by someone prepared to accept responsibility.

## Mechanism

Generation multiplies alternatives and weakens visible proxies such as length and polish. Review work moves downstream unless tools and institutions redesign the workflow around tests, provenance, domain judgment, and explicit acceptance criteria.

## Boundary

Verification is not a single final check. It can include formal tests, source triangulation, situated judgment, adversarial review, affected-party feedback, and post-deployment monitoring. Each method has blind spots and costs.
