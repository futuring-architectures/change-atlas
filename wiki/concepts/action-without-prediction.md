# Action without prediction

## Definition

Action without prediction is a discipline for moving in complex systems without making success depend on a single forecast. It combines stable purpose with reversible experiments, option preservation, feedback, and revision.

## Boundary

It is not action without evidence or direction. Probabilities, scenarios, historical mechanisms, and constraints remain useful; they are treated as aids rather than guarantees.
