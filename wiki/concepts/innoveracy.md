# Innoveracy

## Definition

Innoveracy is ignorance about the concept and role of innovation, especially the conflation of novelty, creation, invention, and innovation. It causes actors to attribute success to the visible new artifact while overlooking adoption, production improvement, diffusion, institutions, and value creation.

## Provenance

Horace Dediu coined the term in a 2014 essay on persistent misunderstanding of innovation.

## Aliases and related terms

- Innovation illiteracy
- Novelty confusion
- Innovation-speak

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Clear vocabulary does not by itself establish causal evidence.
- Definitions of innovation vary legitimately across economics, design, and management.
- The concept should not become a gatekeeping device that excludes public values from innovation debate.
- [[innovation-ecology|Innovation ecology]] expands the unit from artifact to variation, selection, diffusion, maintenance, and renewal.
