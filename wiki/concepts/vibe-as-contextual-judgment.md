# Vibe as contextual judgment

## Definition

Vibe as contextual judgment is the ability to appraise tone, fit, plausibility, coherence, and significance before or beyond formal proof. Generative models can imitate this appraisal, while humans may use it to orient exploration and articulate intent.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Contextual usefulness is not factual reliability.
- Vibe can encode tacit expertise or unexamined prejudice.
- “Precise imagination” does not remove the need for implementation and verification.
- Consequential decisions require accountable evidence, not persuasive atmosphere.
