# The technium

## Definition

The technium is Kevin Kelly's term for the human-made system of interdependent technologies. Tools enable the production and operation of other tools; their combinations create emergent dynamics unavailable to an isolated artifact. Kelly interprets this system as an extension of evolution that expands available forms, options, and possibilities.

## Provenance

In the 2023 Noah Smith interview, Kelly defines technologies as mutually supporting and describes the technium as an ecosystem of inventions capable of reaching forms that biological evolution alone cannot. He then equates expanded options and possibilities with progress.

## Aliases and related terms

- Technological ecosystem
- Technosphere
- System of technologies
- Technology as an evolutionary extension

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Interdependence and emergent behavior do not establish consciousness, moral agency, or a unitary objective.
- A system tendency can result from institutions and incentives rather than from technology independent of humans.
- Increasing options is not automatically progress when access, risk, reversibility, and distribution differ.
- The Concept is a provenance-bearing interpretive frame, not an observed natural law.

## Relationships

The Concept sits at a higher level than a firm ecosystem: it treats firms, tools, infrastructure, and institutions as participants in one technological system. No typed causal relationship is promoted from this single excerpt.
