# Ingredient technology

## Definition

An ingredient technology supplies a reusable capability but does not define the complete product or outcome. Domain logic, scenario code, data, interface, assurance, policy, and service operations surround it until the ingredient becomes an ordinary feature in a situated system.

## Provenance

Steven Sinofsky proposes that generative-AI functions such as summarization, rewriting, templates, and draft creation may disappear into products as ordinary features, with AI acting as an ingredient rather than a standalone category.

## Aliases and related terms

- Enabling technology
- Embedded capability
- Component technology
- AI inside

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Ingredient does not mean unimportant; a component can still shape cost, architecture, and dependence.
- The concept resists evaluating a whole workflow from model capability alone.
- A model provider may try to move upward from ingredient to product, platform, or control layer.
- Commoditization of the ingredient can increase the value of integration and institutional fit.
