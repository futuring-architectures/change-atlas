# Hierarchy as meaning infrastructure

## Definition

Hierarchy as meaning infrastructure describes the managerial work of interpreting context, connecting decisions to purpose, developing judgment, and helping participants locate themselves in a collective—not merely routing instructions and information.

## Provenance

Nitzan Hermon distinguishes hierarchy's information-routing role from its meaning-scaffolding role. The distinction matters because AI may compress access to information and coordination while leaving interpretation, development, responsibility, and identity unresolved.

## Aliases and related terms

- Meaning scaffolding
- Developmental hierarchy
- Contextual leadership
- Interpretive management

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- The concept does not defend every hierarchy. Formal authority can suppress local knowledge, distort meaning, and protect status.
- Meaning can be produced through peers, craft communities, rituals, narratives, and participation rather than managers alone.
- Information and meaning are not separable in practice: what is routed, omitted, emphasized, and explained already shapes interpretation.
- AI may assist sensemaking and coaching, but assistance does not settle who carries responsibility or whose interpretation governs.
