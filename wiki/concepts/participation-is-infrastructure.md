# Participation is infrastructure

## Definition

In many networks, participants perform functions that become part of the system’s operating infrastructure. Ratings create trust, advice resolves uncertainty, behavior trains ranking, content supplies inventory, and relationships produce community. The platform or protocol is therefore not only code and capital; it is also organized participant activity.

## Provenance

The early eBay case makes the mechanism visible: users supplied ratings, advice, and shipping guidance that increased marketplace usefulness. Tal’s protocol account adds a governance hypothesis: ownership can shift participation from transaction toward community. TikTok supplies a different form—behavioral micro-signals that improve the platform’s allocation system.

## Aliases and related terms

- Community as infrastructure
- User labor
- Participant-produced value
- Prosumer network

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Participation can be voluntary, compensated, extractive, reciprocal, or governed; the Concept does not assume one value distribution.
- Ownership may align participants, but it can also financialize community and privilege capital over contribution.
- User-generated data and community stewardship are both participation, but they confer very different agency.
- A platform can depend on participation while still controlling the rules, data, and proceeds.
