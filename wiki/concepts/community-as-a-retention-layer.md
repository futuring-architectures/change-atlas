# Community as a retention layer

## Definition

Community as a retention layer describes a creator system in which content is the entry point but belonging, identity, peer relationships, and direct access sustain engagement.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Retention is a business outcome; community also has participant-defined purposes that may conflict with growth.
- Not every audience wants membership, interaction, or identity.
- A direct relationship can still depend on payment, hosting, and discovery infrastructure.
- Community language can disguise customers, unpaid labor, or a managed fan base without voice.
