# Taste after technical commoditization

## Definition

Taste after technical commoditization describes the relocation of differentiation from making an artifact possible to deciding what is worth making, selecting among variants, and establishing cultural legitimacy.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Taste is socially produced and can reproduce class, institutional, and demographic power.
- Cheap execution does not eliminate reliability, architecture, or operational expertise.
- Scarcity can shift into status games disconnected from usefulness.
- Culturally low-status AI aesthetics may change as tools and institutions mature.
