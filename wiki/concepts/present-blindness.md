# Present blindness

## Definition

Present blindness is the inability to perceive consequential current change because attention is monocropped, novelty is over-weighted, categories are inherited, and elegant models are defended against contradictory reality.

## Significance

Futuring begins with better observation, not farther projection. Signal curation is therefore an epistemic design problem: which sources, anomalies, affected people, and counterexamples can unsettle the current frame?

## Boundary

No observer sees an unmediated present. The remedy is plural and revisable perception, not a claim to neutrality.
