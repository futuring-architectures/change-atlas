# Task-boundary work

## Definition

Task-boundary work is the interpretation, coordination, exception handling, negotiation, and specification required between discrete tasks. It is often difficult to benchmark precisely because the absence of a precise specification is part of what makes the job complex.

## Provenance

Arvind Narayanan uses radiology to argue that task decompositions can define away the nuanced, hard-to-automate work at task boundaries.

## Aliases and related terms

- Boundary work
- Inter-task coordination
- Workflow glue
- Ambiguity work

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Not all boundary work is valuable; some reflects bad process or missing interfaces.
- Boundary work can become more legible and automatable over time.
- Benchmark success remains useful evidence about a bounded task.
- A job is not reducible to the sum of tasks if coordination changes their meaning and consequence.
