# Extended mind

## Definition

Extended-mind theory treats thinking as distributed across biological and external resources rather than contained entirely inside an individual's brain. Generative AI can therefore be analyzed as a component in a hybrid cognitive system, alongside language, notebooks, collaborators, institutions, and other tools.

## Provenance

Andy Clark applies the extended-mind tradition to generative AI and argues that accounts beginning with an isolated biological brain start from the wrong unit of analysis.

## Aliases and related terms

- Extended cognition
- Distributed cognition
- Hybrid thinking system
- Cognitive scaffolding

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Extension does not make every tool use part of the mind; reliability, availability, integration, and trust matter.
- Greater system capability does not establish that the individual has acquired durable competence.
- Cognitive extension can increase agency or create dependence, surveillance, and skill atrophy.
- The concept changes the unit of analysis without settling who owns or governs the external components.
