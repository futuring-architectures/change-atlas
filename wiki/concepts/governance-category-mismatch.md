# Governance category mismatch

## Definition

Governance category mismatch occurs when rules inherit a category from an older institutional form even though the governed system combines roles, capabilities, or dynamics that the category does not capture. The result is not necessarily absence of regulation; it can be regulation aimed at the wrong object or mechanism.

The name is an Atlas synthesis. The Evidence describes the underlying problem but does not establish this exact term as a named concept.

## Provenance

- Online speech systems are described as combining aspects of platforms and publishers while being neither, with rules needing to reflect how the new form works: [[online-speech-and-publishing-evi-709567530c09#Claim 1|online speech as a distinct institutional form]] and [[online-speech-and-publishing-evi-709567530c09#Claim 2|rules grounded in system operation]].
- Advanced AI is described as software-like while also having human-associated versatility and unreliability: [[how-do-you-govern-something-that-s-part-software-and-part-people-evi-da73ef066f1e#Claim 3|AI as a hybrid software-and-people category]].
- [[structural-uncertainty|Structural uncertainty]] provides the broader idea that the playing field and object of action can change rather than merely vary within known boundaries: [[chegg-chatgpt-and-the-changing-nature-of-change-evi-90ef6bd085b1#Claim 1|uncertainty that changes the playing field]].
- Rapid technological consequence can compress society's time to understand systems and form regulatory judgments: [[regulating-technology-evi-b7d6baa65d0e#Claim 2|compressed time for regulatory understanding]].
- Conversational interfaces can induce a social category error: natural language primes people to treat a system as person-like and attribute a theory of mind to it: [[ai-and-trust-evi-6f21eb38aa3b#Claim 1|language-induced person categorization]] and [[ai-and-trust-evi-6f21eb38aa3b#Claim 2|attributing a theory of mind to AI]].
- LLM behavior can violate expectations imported from both dependable software and human error: expressed confidence does not separate truth from falsehood, while inconsistent failures undermine multi-step trust: [[ai-mistakes-are-very-different-from-human-mistakes-evi-6ce1d0622be3#Claim 1|uncalibrated expressed confidence]] and [[ai-mistakes-are-very-different-from-human-mistakes-evi-6ce1d0622be3#Claim 2|inconsistent multi-step failure]].
- Mass automation may make a quantitative increase into a different policy object: [[generative-ai-and-intellectual-property-evi-f3ed589d9de7#Claim 2|scale as a possible difference in principle]].

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- A category can remain useful while needing qualification; novelty alone does not prove mismatch.
- Regulatory lag concerns timing. Category mismatch concerns the model of the governed object, though the two can reinforce each other.
- Hybrid systems do not necessarily need an entirely new legal category; a layered combination of existing regimes may be sufficient.
- A misleading interface category and a legal category mismatch are related but distinct: one affects user judgment, the other institutional treatment.
- Difference at scale does not automatically establish difference in principle; the relevant threshold and affected value must be demonstrated.
- The concept diagnoses a problem but does not determine which new rules should apply.
