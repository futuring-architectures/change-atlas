# Liquid Super Team

## Definition

A Liquid Super Team is a proposed project network in which independent specialists temporarily combine capabilities without subsuming their identities into a stable firm.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- It resembles a [[co-acting-group|co-acting group]] but adds an entrepreneurial claim about autonomy and exceptional combined capability.
- “Liquid” can mean flexibility or precarity depending on contracts, benefits, bargaining, and continuity.
- The concept is prospective; the source supplies no evaluated operating case.
- Informal reputation and platform access can recreate hierarchy outside an org chart.
