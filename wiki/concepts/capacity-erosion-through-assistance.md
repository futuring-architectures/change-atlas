# Capacity erosion through assistance

## Definition

Capacity erosion through assistance occurs when a system raises short-term performance while reducing the practice, attention, verification, persistence, or participation through which people remain able to act independently and govern consequences.

## Provenance

Howard Esbin groups persistence, verification, and participation as mutually reinforcing capacities that AI assistance may erode. Venkatesh Rao offers a qualification: the changed attention pattern of prompting can reflect the legitimate shift from doing to managing rather than simple cognitive decline.

## Aliases and related terms

- Assistance-induced deskilling
- Automation complacency
- Human-capacity atrophy
- Participation erosion

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Lower continuous effort is not automatically erosion; it may be delegation to a reliable system.
- Capacity can move upward from execution into orchestration and judgment.
- Independent performance is not the only valuable outcome, but dependence changes bargaining power and resilience.
- Longitudinal measures are required; immediate task scores cannot establish erosion.
