# Narrative as organizational firmware

## Definition

Narrative as organizational firmware treats a firm's underlying story as a slow-changing layer that coordinates identity, priorities, and interpretation. Surface campaigns can be updated quickly; foundational narratives are more deeply embedded and create narrative debt when repeated patches contradict one another.

## Provenance

Paul Worthington names the firmware metaphor and narrative debt. Cory Doctorow's account of growth stories in Big Tech shows why narrative can become economically load-bearing rather than merely expressive.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- Narrative is not evidence. A coherent story can conceal failing operations or redistribute attention away from material problems.
- Durability supports coordination but can harden into ideology and block adaptation.
- Organizations contain multiple publics and counter-narratives; no single story is culturally neutral.
- Authorship and the right to contest the narrative are governance questions, not branding details.
