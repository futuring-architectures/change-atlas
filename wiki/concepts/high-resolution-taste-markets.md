# High-resolution taste markets

## Definition

High-resolution taste markets replace a single broad ranking with many differentiated preference vectors. They can create multiple category leaders and let supply reshape demand rather than merely optimize for an inherited dominant aesthetic.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- More niches do not eliminate power laws; they may reproduce them within smaller categories.
- Personalized discovery can diversify taste or isolate people in manipulable micro-markets.
- Taste resolution does not itself provide income stability, ownership, or portable audience access.
- Creators help form demand, so allocation is cultural governance rather than neutral matching.
