# Platform-brand employment

## Definition

Platform-brand employment treats an employer as infrastructure for an individual's reach and legitimacy, not only as purchaser of labor. The worker gains audience growth; the organization gains a portfolio of visible people.

## Distinctions and contested meanings

- The model can increase worker portability or let employers claim and govern personal reputation.
- Public reach is not relevant or safe in every occupation.
- Audience metrics can distort internal evaluation and intensify self-promotion.
- The source offers a prospective model, not an observed prevalence claim.
