# Intent as executable material

## Definition

Intent becomes executable material when a person can shape system behavior through natural language, examples, data, constraints, and feedback rather than only through code written by a specialist intermediary.

## Mechanism

Language models translate fuzzy direction into provisional executable artifacts. The bottleneck moves from syntax toward specification: who can express the purpose, assemble context, expose exceptions, and determine whether the result fits.

## Boundary

Intent is not self-executing truth. Ambiguity, unstated constraints, security, integration, and maintenance remain. Calling intent “source code” can hide the engineering system that interprets and verifies it.
