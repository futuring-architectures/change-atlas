# Intent as executable material

## Definition

Intent becomes executable material when a person can shape system behavior through natural language, examples, data, constraints, and feedback rather than only through code written by a specialist intermediary.

## Mechanism

Language models translate fuzzy direction into provisional executable artifacts. The bottleneck moves from syntax toward specification: who can express the purpose, assemble context, expose exceptions, and determine whether the result fits.

Robin Sloan's personal notes-app process makes formulation and revision concrete. Before opening the agent, he spent an hour writing references, desired behavior, and exclusions. His specification bounded the work to local text files, standard controls, simple search, and a plan before implementation. After using minimal versions, he identified missing capabilities and removed features that failed in practice. Intent therefore supplied both an initial constraint and a revisable account of fit: [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 8|preparing the vision]], [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 10|bounding the specification]], and [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 14|revision through use]].

## Boundary

Intent is not self-executing truth. Ambiguity, unstated constraints, security, integration, and maintenance remain. Calling intent “source code” can hide the engineering system that interprets and verifies it.
