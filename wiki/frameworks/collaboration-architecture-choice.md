# Collaboration architecture choice

## Purpose

Use this Framework to choose a collaboration form from the work's actual dependencies rather than defaulting to office presence, meetings, stable teams, or AI-assisted individual production.

## The decision sequence

### 1. Locate task interdependence

Can one person produce a bounded result, or must participants continuously adjust to one another? High reciprocal interdependence favors a stable team; low interdependence permits looser forms.

### 2. Locate context volatility

How quickly do assumptions and priorities change? High volatility can require synchronous interpretation even when production itself is independent.

### 3. Locate concentration needs

Which work requires protected focus? Prefer asynchronous artifacts and deliberate contact where meetings primarily satisfy visibility rather than coordination.

### 4. Locate social infrastructure needs

Does the work also develop novices, create trust, negotiate conflict, distribute recognition, or build identity? These needs can justify durable human contact beyond immediate task efficiency.

### 5. Choose the lightest sufficient form

- **Focused individual or AI-assisted work** for bounded production with low interdependence.
- **Asynchronous coordination** when shared artifacts can carry context without rapid mutual adjustment.
- **Co-acting group** when specialized contributors enter and leave around modules or phases.
- **Stable team** when joint mission, reciprocal interdependence, trust, and shared learning must persist.

### 6. Preserve escalation and review

Name who integrates outputs, resolves conflict, verifies AI assistance, and can convene richer collaboration when the chosen form fails.

## Applicability and limitations

- The Framework is a choice aid, not evidence that one form is globally more productive.
- Different phases of the same initiative may require different forms.
- Individual efficiency can externalize repair, mentoring, and coordination costs to others.
- Social contact should not become compulsory performance or a substitute for accessible systems and clear authority.
