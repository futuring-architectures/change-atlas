# Practical creator arc

## Purpose

Use this Framework to design a creative transition without treating immediate monetization or a dramatic leap as proof of commitment.

## The arc

1. **Fund attention:** use employment, savings, patronage, or other income to create protected time and reduce survival pressure.
2. **Develop the practice:** build skill, a body of work, and feedback while costs remain bounded.
3. **Test direct value:** learn which people return, pay, recommend, or participate without confusing reach with durable demand.
4. **Cross deliberately:** shift time only when runway, recurring support, and operating capacity make the risk explicit.
5. **Preserve the practice:** reinvest in attention, boundaries, learning, and resilience rather than maximizing output and audience demands.

## Limitations

- Access to savings, paid work, time, and safe experimentation is unequally distributed.
- The arc is a planning model, not evidence that persistence guarantees a market.
- Financial security can enable work but does not determine artistic quality.
- Care duties, disability, geography, and discrimination materially shape feasible paths.
