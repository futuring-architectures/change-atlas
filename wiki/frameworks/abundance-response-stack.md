# Abundance response stack

## Purpose

Use this Framework when production is no longer the binding constraint and adding output risks worsening the system.

## The seven layers

1. **Formulate:** define the problem and affected parties before generating.
2. **Verify:** separate contextual plausibility from facts and consequential claims.
3. **Select:** articulate taste, quality, fit, and exclusion criteria.
4. **Establish trust:** preserve provenance, disclosure, reputation, and correction.
5. **Slow down:** allow comparison, discussion, incubation, and revision.
6. **Subtract:** remove options, features, and stimuli that do not serve the purpose.
7. **Assign responsibility:** name who has the authority, competence, and obligation to own the outcome.

## How to apply it

Trace an artifact from request to outcome. At each layer, record who performs the work, what evidence they use, what can be contested, and whether labor shifts downstream. If a layer is automated, test whether capability and accountability moved with it.

## Limitations

- Slowness and curation can become privilege or gatekeeping.
- Human judgment can be biased and unaccountable.
- Scarcity relocation does not make production skill irrelevant.
- The layers interact and may repeat rather than form a linear process.
