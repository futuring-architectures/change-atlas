# Does cheap creation democratize expression or cheapen culture?

## Position A

Lower technical friction lets people without professional training externalize imagination, enter creative work, and experiment at new scale.

## Position B

Radical overproduction rewards speed, imitation, and disposability; characteristic AI aesthetics become low-status because they are cheap and ubiquitous.

## Position C

The outcome depends on institutions around production: incentives, source compensation, curation, provenance, learning, and time for heavier work.

## Current assessment

Access has widened in bounded cases. Cultural democratization, quality, livelihood, and diversity are separate outcomes and remain unresolved.
