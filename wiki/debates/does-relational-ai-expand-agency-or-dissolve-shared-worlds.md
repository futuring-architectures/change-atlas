# Does relational AI expand agency or dissolve shared worlds?

## Position: relational AI expands agency

Natural interaction lowers technical barriers, makes information from many people accessible, and can operate as a perspective-generating partner rather than an oracle. Shared AI surfaces could support multiple participants around a common problem.

## Position: relational AI dissolves shared worlds

Anthropomorphic design encourages false beliefs about intention and credibility. Persistent agents can produce emotional dependency, while personalized synthetic media can move people into separate reality channels and offload democratic deliberation.

## Synthesis

The key design variable is whether relationship becomes a bridge among people and evidence or a privately optimized substitute for them. Shared surfaces, provenance, plural participation, portability, and limits on manipulative attachment are institutional requirements, not interface polish.
