# Does cheap software distribute agency or relocate control?

## Position: agency distributes

Natural language reduces the translation barrier between domain intent and executable behavior. Employees can build their own tools, users can shape persistent agent instructions, and bespoke software can replace fixed bundles designed for an imagined average user.

The reported CAD case supplies a concrete version of that distribution: a person who could not operate the specialist tool directed an agent through natural language to produce a design. The broader claim that this can extend to any software remains a forecast rather than a comparative adoption result.

## Position: control relocates

The interpreter is still owned. Model providers, agent platforms, tool permissions, distribution systems, and data schemas can become stronger control points than the application layer they displace. Users may inherit responsibility for systems they cannot inspect, export, or repair.

## Synthesis

### Construction and runtime control

Sloan separates depending on an agent to build software from depending on one whenever the software runs. He reports daily use of a personal notes app built in one evening and advocates finished tools that keep working without runtime AI calls. This offers one way to limit exposure to model outages, price changes, and shifting responses, while locating value in personal fit and control over changes: [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 2|the reported notes-app case]], [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 4|standalone tools as the goal]], [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 17|runtime dependency risks]], and [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 21|personal fit and change authority]].

Control over runtime behavior is not the same as independent repair. Sloan already knows how to program but says he does not inspect the generated code; he also describes refinement as a hobby requiring quiet time. The case therefore distinguishes desired fit, independence from model calls, and the capacity and time to maintain a tool: [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 16|prior expertise and code-review practice]] and [[ask-for-what-you-want-is-a-key-skill-for-the-21st-century-evi-1ec76f5e388c#Claim 15|time and participation]].

### Conditions for distributed agency

Cheap production distributes capability more readily than it distributes institutional agency. The outcome depends on portability, editable state, permission design, skill formation, value capture, and recourse—the dimensions in the [[ai-agency-audit|AI agency audit]].

Agent mediation also redistributes interface complexity. [[previously-unmanufacturable-evi-4d49249b45d7#Claim 4|Previously Unmanufacturable — claim 4]] places schemas, documentation, and safe action in the agent-facing harness; that can simplify the human surface without settling who can inspect, authorize, repair, or exit the resulting system.

## Evidence needed

- Who builds and maintains user-authored tools after initial generation.
- Comparative switching, export, and failure-recovery outcomes across agent platforms.
- Whether domain users gain decision rights or only assume more unpaid configuration and review work.
- Whether tools remain usable without the building agent, and whether their users can recover, repair, or migrate them when maintenance is needed.
