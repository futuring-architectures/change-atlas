# Do niches democratize creative opportunity?

## Position A

Creator infrastructure lowers entry barriers, plural taste systems create more kinds of winners, and direct audiences reduce reliance on institutional gatekeepers.

## Position B

Niches fragment markets without changing power-law rewards; creators remain dependent on platforms and must turn identity and leisure into continuous competitive labor.

## Position C

Democratization must be decomposed into entry, discovery, income, ownership, mobility, and durability. A system can improve one while worsening another.

## Current assessment

The corpus provides mechanisms and selected successes, not representative income or mobility outcomes. “More creators” and “more sustainable creator careers” are separate propositions.
