# Does AI progress require scaling or new paradigms?

## Position: the current paradigm still has headroom

Model cost and benchmark performance improved dramatically, including small open models overtaking an early GPT-4 release. Application development remains behind capabilities already available, so disappointing products do not demonstrate exhausted technical progress.

## Position: a qualitative break is required

Efficient human-like learning, learning-to-learn, and paradigm-generating science may not emerge from adding specialized capability or scaling present architectures. Current systems can improve inside “normal science” while remaining structurally unable to create new frames.

## Synthesis

The two positions operate at different levels. Current systems can improve and diffuse rapidly while still lacking the mechanism required for a more general transition. Evaluation must specify which capability, cost, interface, and application outcome is claimed.

## Evidence needed

- Measures resistant to benchmark saturation and contamination.
- Application outcomes linked to particular capability changes.
- Tests of transfer, causal learning, and frame creation rather than fluent task completion.
