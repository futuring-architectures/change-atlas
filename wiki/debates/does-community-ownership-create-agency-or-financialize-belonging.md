# Does community ownership create agency or financialize belonging?

## Central question

When cultural participation receives ownership or tradable value, does it distribute power and upside—or recast identity, taste, and relationships as speculative assets?

## Position A

Ownership can turn participants from audience or unpaid infrastructure into stakeholders with aligned incentives, voice, and shared upside.

## Position B

Financialization privileges capital, timing, and speculation; it can crowd out intrinsic motivation and make belonging contingent on a market position.

## Position C

The outcome depends on governance design: transferability, voting, contribution recognition, concentration limits, exit, and recourse matter more than the presence of a token.

## Current assessment

The corpus supplies competing mechanisms and forecasts but no comparative community outcomes. Ownership, agency, and financial return must be measured separately.
