## Curated excerpt

> *\[S\]tatistical inference is surely not the whole story of human cooperation, creativity, coordination, and competition. Have we become so mesmerized by Large Language Models that we do not see the rest of what we do in language? \[…\] We build organizations and societies. We create traditions and histories. We take responsibility for actions. We build trust \[…\] None of these is statistical. There is a great chasm between the capabilities of LLMs and those of human beings.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Human language participates in cooperation, creativity, responsibility, trust, institutions, and history in ways that cannot be reduced to statistical inference.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Differences between language-model output and human social action.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-06-16.
- Qualification: Many human activities also contain statistical regularities; the claim concerns what statistical generation alone does not establish.

### Claim 2

There is a large gap between the capabilities of large language models and the socially situated capabilities of human beings.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Capabilities of LLMs relative to humans as of June 2023.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-06-16.
- Qualification: The comparison spans unlike categories and is time-bound as systems and human-machine arrangements change.
