## Curated excerpt

> *The most important insight from the o1 paper is that there’s a new scaling law in town. Pre-training LLMs follows a well understood scaling law: the more compute and data you spend on pre-training the model, the better it performs. The o1 paper has opened up an entire new plane for scaling compute: the more inference-time (or “test-time”)  compute you give the model, the better it reasons.*
