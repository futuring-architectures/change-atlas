## Curated excerpt

> *Power is a part of any technology because power is required to build that technology in the first place. Power becomes more entrenched into a system when it is built with power as its organizing principle, or value. In a design process, there are countless number of decisions that require trade-offs \[…\] resolved by thinking about that central value. If it's power, then that's how it gets resolved.*
