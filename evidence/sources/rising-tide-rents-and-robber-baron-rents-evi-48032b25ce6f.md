## Curated excerpt

> *In the dying stages of the old cycle, the companies on top of the heap turn to extractive techniques, using their market power to try to maintain their now-customary level of profits in the face of macroeconomic factors and competition that ought to be eating them away. They start to collect robber baron rents. That’s exactly what Google, Amazon, and Meta are doing today.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Late in a business cycle, incumbent firms use market power and extractive techniques to defend profits that competition and macroeconomic change would otherwise erode.

- Scope: Rent extraction by dominant firms in a mature market cycle.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-04-23.
- Qualification: The cyclical model does not by itself distinguish extraction from defensible pricing or investment recovery.
