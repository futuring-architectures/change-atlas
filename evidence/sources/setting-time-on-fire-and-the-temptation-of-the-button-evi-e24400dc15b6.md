## Curated excerpt

> *AI will make a lot of previously useful tasks meaningless. It it will also remove the facade that previously disguised meaningless tasks. We may not have always known if our work mattered in the bigger picture, but in most organizations, the people in your part of the organizational structure felt that it did. With AI-generated work sent to other AIs to assess, that sense of meaning disappears.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI can make previously useful tasks meaningless and expose tasks whose organizational value was already largely performative.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Knowledge-work tasks affected by generative AI.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2023-06-03.
- Qualification: Task value depends on purpose and context; apparent automability does not establish reliable substitution or lack of developmental value.

### Claim 2

When AI-generated work is assessed by other AIs, workers can lose the social recognition that made organizational work feel meaningful.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Meaning in AI-mediated production and evaluation chains.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2023-06-03.
- Qualification: Meaning can also arise from autonomy, mastery, beneficiaries, craft, peers, and purposes beyond immediate human review.
