## Curated excerpt

> *Machines are strong at induction, workable at structured deduction, and strong at the generative side of abduction. They are weak at the selective side of abduction, the cognitive act of choosing which new framing is worth committing to, because they have no skin in the game and no cost for being wrong. That act is irreducibly human, and most of the other choreography follows from this one distinction.*
