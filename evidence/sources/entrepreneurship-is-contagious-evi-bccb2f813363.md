## Curated excerpt

> *I suspect entrepreneurship is only “contagious” to those who normally wouldn’t consider it. Maybe, for most people, starting a new business is literally unthinkable, in the sense that they just don’t think of doing it. But being around someone who has done it plants the seed in your mind that it’s a possibility, something you really could do \[..\] For them, exposure has a measurable positive effect.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Exposure to entrepreneurs has a measurable positive effect on entrepreneurship for people who otherwise might not consider starting a business.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Research reviewed by the author on peer exposure and entrepreneurship.

### Claim 2

Communities can expand agency by making previously unimagined forms of action cognitively available through nearby examples.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Social exposure as a mechanism for entrepreneurial possibility.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-08-05.
- Qualification: Exposure also transmits selection bias, risk normalization, and local opportunity structures.
