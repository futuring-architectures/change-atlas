## Curated excerpt

> *Rather than having a human write instructions for the computer to follow, training works by searching through a space of possible programs until we find one that works. In Software 2.0, problems of science—which is about formal theories and rules—become problems of engineering, which is about accomplishing an outcome. This shift—from science to engineering—will have a massive impact on how we think about solving problems, and how we understand the world.*
