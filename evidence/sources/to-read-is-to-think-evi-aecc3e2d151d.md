## Curated excerpt

> *To read is to think. Only at inference is there space for serendipitous inspiration, which is why LLMs have so little of it to show for all they’ve seen. My point is training leaves data undigested. Were a model to ponder the implications of every pair of documents in its corpora and train on those it deemed good, it would presumably learn a lot.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Model training leaves source data insufficiently digested because it lacks inference-time-like reflection across documents.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Difference between language-model training and deliberative synthesis.
- Date relevance: Claim posted 2024-12-14.
- Qualification: Training already combines statistical evidence across data; “undigested” is a metaphor for limited explicit cross-document reflection.

### Claim 2

A model that evaluated implications across pairs of documents and trained on selected syntheses could learn more than one trained only on the original corpus.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: A proposed reflective or synthetic-data training process.
