## Curated excerpt

> *Large Models are themselves best understood as a particular type of cultural and social technology. They are analogous to such past technologies as writing, print, markets, bureaucracies, and representative democracies \[…\] Many past cultural and social technologies also had profound, transformative effects on societies, for good and ill, and this is likely to be true for Large Models.*
