## Curated excerpt

> *Humans in the loop experience "a diminished sense of control, responsibility, and moral agency." \[…\] Remember, the whole reason to introduce AI is because of human imperfection. Designing an AI to correct human imperfection that only works when its human overseer is perfect produces predictably bad outcomes.*
