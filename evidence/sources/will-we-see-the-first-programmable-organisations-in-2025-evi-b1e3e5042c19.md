## Curated excerpt

> *Organisations can be more automated and machine-like at the back-end platform level in order to be more human and connected at the front end. It will lead to an unbundling and re-bundling of 'jobs' and roles; many will no longer be needed, others will become more interesting and valued, and new roles and opportunities will emerge for those willing to engage with change.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Organizations may automate their back-end platforms in order to support more human connection at the front end.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Programmable organizations.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2025-01-02.
- Qualification: Automation does not automatically produce more humane interaction.

### Claim 2

Programmable organizations will unbundle and recombine jobs, removing some roles while increasing the value of others and creating new ones.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Organizational design under automation.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2025-01-02.
- Qualification: The timing, distribution, and net employment effects are uncertain.
