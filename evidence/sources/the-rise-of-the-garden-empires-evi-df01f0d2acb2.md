## Curated excerpt

> *Language, especially poetry and myth, allowed individuals to pass down their wisdom at high fidelity to become the wisdom of the group. This meant that man could run circles around other beasts by means of a much faster accumulation of knowledge. Where evolution picks up a few bits of information every generation at best, cultured humans can build on their ancestors’ wisdom and create whole new engines of cultural machinery in a single generation.*
