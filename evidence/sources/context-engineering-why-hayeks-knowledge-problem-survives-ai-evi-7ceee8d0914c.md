## Curated excerpt

> *A new kind of knowledge worker: one who has done enough of the underlying work to know what good looks like, and whose value lies in framing the right problems, prioritizing what data needs to be captured, curating and assembling it for AI consumption, and evaluating whether the AI output actually makes sense.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Context engineering requires enough underlying experience to frame problems, prioritize data, curate inputs, and judge outputs.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Knowledge work mediated by AI systems.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2026-03-04.
- Qualification: Teams can distribute these capabilities rather than require them in one worker.

### Claim 2

Tacit domain judgment remains valuable because AI cannot determine which available context matters without human framing.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Applying Hayek’s knowledge problem to AI-assisted work.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2026-03-04.
- Qualification: Models can increasingly retrieve and rank context, but their criteria still require governance and evaluation.
