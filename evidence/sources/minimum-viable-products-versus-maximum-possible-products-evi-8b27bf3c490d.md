## Curated excerpt

> *Maximum possible product’s, or MPPs, are not about idea validation to the entrepreneur, but rather to the masses. MPPs require very high execution because they are trying to convince people that they need something they previously didn’t know they did. Any sort of mediocrity in the product is doing a disservice to convincing them that they need this product.*

### Claim 1
