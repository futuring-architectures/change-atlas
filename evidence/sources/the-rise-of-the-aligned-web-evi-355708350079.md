## Curated excerpt

> *The \[..\] great thing about the aligned web is that everybody can see who's paying for what. None of the fees are invisible — it's all public and tamper-proof because it's on-chain. Unlike the adversarial web, the aligned web isn't incentivized to lie to any stakeholder about what's really going on \[..\] — no data brokers, experiments, engagement metrics, and so on. That stuff isn’t necessary, so it isn’t there.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

On-chain payments can make who pays whom public and resistant to tampering.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Payments recorded on transparent public blockchains.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-11-11.
- Qualification: Public visibility and tamper resistance vary by chain, tooling, address attribution, and privacy techniques.

### Claim 2

The source argues that transparent on-chain payment relationships remove incentives or need for hidden data-brokerage, experimentation, and engagement-metric practices.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Blockchain-supported online services whose revenue relationships are visible on-chain.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-11-11.
- Qualification: This is a broad causal claim; transparent payments do not by themselves eliminate incentives for surveillance, experimentation, or engagement optimization.
