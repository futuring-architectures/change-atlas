## Curated excerpt

> *I’m of the belief that “Come for the Content, Stay for the Community” will be one of the dominating themes for media this decade .. So it’s not a question of “how many newsletters can one person pay for?” it’s “how many communities does someone want to be a part of?”*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Content can function as the entry point to a durable community relationship rather than the complete product.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Paid media, newsletters, games, and other creator-led audiences.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2020-10-19.
- Qualification: Many audiences want content without identity, interaction, or ongoing community obligations.

### Claim 2

The addressable limit for paid creator media may depend more on how many communities people join than how many newsletters they buy.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Consumer demand for creator-led paid media during the 2020s.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2020-10-19.
- Qualification: Attention, income, switching, moderation quality, and community overlap can bind before willingness to belong.
