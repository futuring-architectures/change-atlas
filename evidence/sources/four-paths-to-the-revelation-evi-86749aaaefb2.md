> *One problem with AI is its tendency to hallucinate, making up fake facts that seem entirely justified. This is a problem if you are not an expert in the field the AI is addressing, but it also means that the AI is capable of tremendous world-building. You can work together to go as deep into a world as you would like \[…\] If you hit a wall in your explorations now, it likely won’t exist sometime in the next year. And that is a revelatory realization.*
