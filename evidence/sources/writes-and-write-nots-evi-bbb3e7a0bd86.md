## Curated excerpt

> *Almost all pressure to write has dissipated. You can have AI do it for you, both in school and at work. The result will be a world divided into writes and write-nots \[…\] Instead of good writers, ok writers, and people who can't write, there will just be good writers and people who can't write \[…\] It will be a world of thinks and think-nots. I know which half I want to be in, and I bet you do too.*
