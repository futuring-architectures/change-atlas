## Curated excerpt

> *There is no point where learning disparate skills —object detection, speech synthesis, chess playing, … you name it— suddenly leads us to learn how to learn. There’s a qualitative jump there, in the same sense as making ever higher skyscrapers won’t lead us to the moon. It’s not just a matter of scaling what has worked so far. You need a completely different theory.*
