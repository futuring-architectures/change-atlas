## Curated excerpt

> *Google didn’t know that asking Paul Buchheit to tinker with email would end up producing Gmail or that hiring Krishna Bharat would ultimately lead to the invention and development of Google News \[..\] The world of Ford is the world of interchangeable employees working on clearly defined tasks. The world of Google is a world of specific employees working on loosely or undefined tasks.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Google produced major products by giving particular employees room to explore loosely defined work.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Reported origins of Gmail and Google News.

- Qualification: Selected successes do not reveal the failure rate of open-ended exploration.

### Claim 2

Innovation under uncertainty depends on specific people pursuing ambiguous tasks rather than interchangeable workers executing fixed ones.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Exploratory organizational work.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-07-02.
- Qualification: Defined tasks and repeatable processes remain essential in other parts of an organization.
