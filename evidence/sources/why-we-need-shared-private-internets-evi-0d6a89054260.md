## Curated excerpt

> *We’ve gotten so used to the default of thinking infinite scale is a good thing, we’ve come to think of privacy as a bad thing. But wanting privacy isn’t a retreat. Craving more space for your ideas doesn’t mean you’re doing something dangerous. Privacy is about having agency over your own thoughts and words, and how far they spread. Privacy is about control.*

### Claim 1

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Cultural assumptions about scale and privacy in online publishing.

- Qualification: Privacy norms vary by context, and concern about secrecy can arise from safety and accountability needs as well as scale ideology.

### Claim 2

Privacy is agency over one's thoughts and words, including control over how far they spread.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: A control-centered definition of privacy.
- Date relevance: Source published 2026-04-22.
- Qualification: Once information is shared with others, control can be partial and relational rather than absolute.
