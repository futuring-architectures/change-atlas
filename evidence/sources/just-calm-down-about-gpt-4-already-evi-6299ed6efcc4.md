## Curated excerpt

> *The large language models are a little surprising. I’ll give you that. And I think what they say, interestingly, is how much of our language is very much rote, R-O-T-E, rather than generated directly, because it can be collapsed down to this set of parameters \[..\] one of the deadly sins was how we humans mistake performance for competence \[..\] our models for generalizing from a performance to a competence don’t apply to AI systems.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The compressibility of language by large models suggests that much human language use is rote rather than freshly reasoned.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Implications of large-language-model performance for human language production.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-05-17.
- Qualification: Model compression and prediction do not by themselves determine the cognitive process behind human utterances.

### Claim 2

People systematically risk mistaking an AI system’s task performance for underlying competence.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Human evaluation of AI capabilities.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-05-17.
- Qualification: The warning does not imply that observed performance is irrelevant; it cautions against unsupported generalization.
