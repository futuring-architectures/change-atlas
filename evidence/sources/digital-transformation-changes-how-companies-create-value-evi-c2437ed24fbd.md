## Curated excerpt

> *Adding the ability to coordinate value creation and exchange — from user to user, partner to partner, and partner to user — is one way that traditional firms transform. It also provides means to scale. Transforming atoms to bits improves margins and reach. Transforming from inside to outside magnifies ideas and resources.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Digital coordination can turn a firm into a platform that scales user-to-user, partner-to-partner, and partner-to-user value exchange.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Platform-oriented digital transformation of firms.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-12-17.
- Qualification: Not every firm has viable network effects, and platform coordination introduces governance, dependency, and quality-control costs.

### Claim 2

Moving value creation from inside the firm to outside participants can magnify accessible ideas and resources beyond the gains from converting atoms to bits alone.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: The inverted-firm or platform transformation described by the authors.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2021-12-17.
- Qualification: External participation does not guarantee value capture, equitable distribution, or defensible advantage.
