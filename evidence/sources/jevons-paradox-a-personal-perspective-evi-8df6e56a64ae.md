## Curated excerpt

> *Integrating AI into our workflows has created a "meta-optimization problem." When everyone suddenly gets 10x more powerful, the hard part isn't doing things—deciding what's worth doing in the first place \[…\] This shift in what we optimize for doesn't mean rejecting productivity gains. Rather, it means capturing those gains as genuine improvements to human flourishing instead of feeding them back into an endless cycle of escalation.*
