## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

For Llama to become a durable platform, it needs a surrounding ecosystem of tools, efficiency improvements, silicon optimization, and integrations built by multiple organizations.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Meta's stated ecosystem requirement for Llama in July 2024.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-07-23.
- Qualification: This is the platform sponsor's strategy claim and does not show that the required complements will emerge or remain open.

### Claim 2

Use by multiple companies can create complements that would not develop around a model used only inside Meta, avoiding the limitations of closed proprietary Unix variants.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Meta's analogy between open-source AI ecosystems and operating-system history.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2024-07-23.
- Qualification: The Unix analogy may not transfer because model training, licensing, compute, safety obligations, and complement economics differ.
