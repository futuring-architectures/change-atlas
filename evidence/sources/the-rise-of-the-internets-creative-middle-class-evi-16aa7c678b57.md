## Curated excerpt

> *Perhaps \[..\] Web 3.0 will play a role in the future of online creative careers, but it’s also clear that Ball and Enjeti, Hilton Carter, Maria Popova, and any number of successful podcasters didn’t depend on a technological breakthrough \[..\] The key to their success seems instead to be the willingness of their audiences to step outside of the algorithmically controlled streams and interact with creators directly, using more varied and independent tools.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Several successful independent creators built durable careers by persuading audiences to leave algorithmic feeds and engage through direct, varied tools.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Selected creator careers described in 2022.
- Date relevance: Contemporaneous cases published 2022-06-15.
- Qualification: The cases are selected successes and do not estimate the size, income stability, or accessibility of a creative middle class.

### Claim 2

Direct audience relationships may matter more to sustainable creator careers than a specific Web3 breakthrough.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Independent online creative careers.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2022-06-15.
- Qualification: Direct tools still depend on payment, hosting, discovery, and platform infrastructure and may not overcome audience concentration.
