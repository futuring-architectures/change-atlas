## Curated excerpt

> *First the internet reduced the marginal cost of distributing content to zero. Now AI is reducing the marginal cost of producing content to zero. Content has become like clay. LLMs can remix it, summarize it, elaborate on it, hallucinate it, combine it with other content, freely transform it between text, audio, image, and back again. It seems we have achieved a kind of information post-scarcity. A regime of radical overproduction. A content singularity.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

LLMs sharply reduce the marginal cost of producing and transforming content, extending the internet’s distribution abundance into production.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Generative content production with LLMs.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2023-01-09.
- Qualification: Compute, verification, source creation, rights, attention, and high-quality judgment remain scarce.

Near-zero production cost creates radical information overproduction rather than automatically useful abundance.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Information environments saturated with generated content.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2023-01-09.
- Qualification: Demand, filtering, norms, regulation, and provenance determine realized information conditions.
