## Curated excerpt

> *\[S\]ubstack is a centralized system. And a centralized system that doesn’t do trust & safety… is the Nazi bar. And if you have some other system that you think allows for “moderation but not censorship” then be fucking explicit about what it is. There are all sorts of interventions short of removing content that have been shown to work well (though, with other social media, they still get accused of “censorship” for literally expressing more speech). But the details matter. A lot.*

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`

- Qualification: The 'Nazi bar' analogy is a warning about selection dynamics, not evidence that every minimally moderated service reaches the same state.

- Scope: Transparency and design of platform moderation systems.

- Qualification: Non-removal interventions can still affect reach and participation and therefore require explicit governance and evaluation.
