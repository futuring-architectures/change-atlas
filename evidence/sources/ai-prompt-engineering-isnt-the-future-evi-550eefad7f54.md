## Curated excerpt

> *\[W\]hat is a more enduring and adaptable skill that will keep enabling us to harness the potential of generative AI? It is problem formulation — the ability to identify, analyze, and delineate problems \[..\] The fact is, without a well-formulated problem, even the most sophisticated prompts will fall short. However, once a problem is clearly defined, the linguistics nuances of a prompt become tangential to the solution.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Problem formulation is more durable than prompt phrasing because it identifies, analyzes, and delineates the problem to be solved.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Human skills for effective generative-AI use.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2023-06-06.
- Qualification: Prompting, domain knowledge, evaluation, and system constraints remain relevant after formulation.

### Claim 2

Sophisticated prompts cannot compensate for a poorly formulated problem.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Generative-AI problem solving.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2023-06-06.
- Qualification: Exploratory use can help formulate problems iteratively rather than requiring a complete formulation first.
