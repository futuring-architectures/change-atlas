## Curated excerpt

> *AI doesn’t just automate tasks but also blends roles, making each team member’s contributions more interdisciplinary. This concept, seen in startups and increasingly in larger companies, suggests a shift where middle managers might need to evolve into roles that amalgamate leadership, product development, and direct customer engagement.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI adoption may blur formal roles by enabling more interdisciplinary contributions across organizational functions.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Knowledge-work organizations adopting generative AI.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-02-21.
- Qualification: Professional boundaries can persist because of expertise, regulation, accountability, labor agreements, and coordination needs.

### Claim 2

Middle managers may evolve into hybrid roles combining people leadership with product, customer, and commercial responsibilities.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: The future role of middle management in AI-enabled organizations.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-02-21.
- Qualification: AI could also increase management, assurance, and translation work or eliminate particular roles; the proposed hybrid is not observed prevalence.
