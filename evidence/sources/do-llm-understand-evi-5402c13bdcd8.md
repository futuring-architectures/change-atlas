## Curated excerpt

> *"Do LLM understand?" is a question that yields passionate answers. As for me and my house: no, LLMs do not reason and in fact are architectural incapable of reasoning \[…\] understanding involves the creation of a theory that can be tested; one consequence of this position is that it requires an understanding (the recursion is intentional) of the limits of that theory \[…\] LLMs give us the feeling of understanding. Sometimes, that is enough.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

LLMs are architecturally incapable of reasoning because understanding requires forming a testable theory and recognizing that theory’s limits.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: The author’s criterion for reasoning and understanding in large language models.
- Date relevance: Claim posted 2023-06-27.
- Qualification: This is a contested philosophical and architectural position, not an agreed capability benchmark.

### Claim 2

LLMs can produce a feeling of understanding that may be sufficient for some uses even if they do not understand.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Human experience of interacting with language models.
- Date relevance: Claim posted 2023-06-27.
- Qualification: Pragmatic sufficiency depends on the task and does not settle whether the system understands.
