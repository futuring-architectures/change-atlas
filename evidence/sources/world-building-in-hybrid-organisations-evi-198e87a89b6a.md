## Curated excerpt

> *A key point often missing from this office vs ‘remote’ working debate is that it is not just about the physical location of work, but rather how we work and how we connect and coordinate with each other. It is about smart, online-first working versus manual, analogue work processes that require an army of management ‘water carriers’ to join things together.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Hybrid work is primarily a coordination and operating-model challenge rather than a choice of physical location.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Hybrid knowledge-work organizations.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-04-30.
- Qualification: Physical location still affects access, trust, informal learning, and the feasibility of particular tasks.

### Claim 2

Online-first processes can reduce dependence on managers who manually carry information between organizational units.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Organizations replacing analogue coordination with shared digital systems.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2021-04-30.
- Qualification: Digital systems can create new integration, maintenance, surveillance, and exception-handling work.
