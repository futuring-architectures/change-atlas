## Curated excerpt

> *Most of the technical people you are hiring are your “programming language feeders”. These are people who take the logic from you. They do not determine the logic. And they will translate it into a language that the computer understands. AI can naturally do that, which is why non-technical people who get exposed to the coding abilities of an LLM get so super excited. You know, like, “oh, I have found my technical co-founder”.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Many software workers translate logic defined by others into programming languages rather than determine the logic themselves.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Organizational division of software work.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2024-08-23.
- Qualification: Developers often contribute domain judgment, architecture, debugging, negotiation, and discovery beyond translation.

### Claim 2

Language models can replace part of the logic-to-code translation that made technical intermediaries necessary.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Software development and nontechnical entrepreneurship.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-08-23.
- Qualification: Generated systems still require specification, testing, security, integration, and accountability.
