## Curated excerpt

> *Chernobyl had a safety handbook. It was violated for understandable organizational reasons and incentives, so I would not exactly blame the reactor control crew. But the written safety rules did need to get violated before the reactor exploded…The AI industry is nowhere near having that.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The AI industry lacks a mature safety handbook comparable to established high-risk engineering fields.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: AI safety practice in 2025.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2025-07-12.
- Qualification: AI systems differ from reactors and some standards and evaluations already exist.

### Claim 2

Organizational incentives can defeat written safety rules, but having operational rules is still a prerequisite for accountable failure analysis.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Safety governance in complex organizations.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2025-07-12.
- Qualification: Rules alone cannot substitute for culture, enforcement, design, and independent oversight.
