## Curated excerpt

> *I don't think people have fully internalized the consequences of this simple fact: any behavior that can be described on a computer, and for which it is possible in principle to collect enough data or evaluate the result automatically, will be doable by AI in short order. This was maybe not as obvious ten years ago, or perhaps even five years ago. Today it is blindingly, fully obvious.*
