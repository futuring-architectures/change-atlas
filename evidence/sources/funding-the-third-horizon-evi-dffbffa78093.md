## Curated excerpt

> *Whatever the sector or context there’s always a ‘business as usual’ dominant pattern. Often if you metaphorically stand in that place and anticipate the future potential you can see how many of the ‘business as usual’ systems are going to fail \[..\] It’s not because anyone is doing anything wrong (although some people are\!), it’s just that the world is changing, and nothing lasts forever.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Business as usual is the dominant first-horizon pattern of activity, infrastructure, and belief that begins to fail as its surrounding world changes.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Three Horizons analysis of established systems and organizations.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-01-24.
- Qualification: The horizon framing is a sensemaking model, not a measured stage sequence, and different participants may place the same activity differently.

### Claim 2

A dominant system can become unfit because its environment changes even when its participants have not acted wrongly.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Institutional and organizational systems under changing external conditions.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2022-01-24.
- Qualification: Environmental change can coexist with design failures, power asymmetries, negligence, or deliberate resistance inside the system.
