## Curated excerpt

> *What will happen as the generations that grew up with ecommerce no longer see it as new and exciting but instead internalise it, and take ownership? Retail is pop culture, and that’s live streaming but it’s also the shop that only you know about. Maybe the internet is due for a wave of things that don’t scale at all. This is retail as anti-logistics \- as a reaction against the firehose.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

As ecommerce becomes ordinary, consumers may seek distinctive retail experiences that resist logistical scale.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Post-ecommerce retail culture.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2021-02-06.
- Qualification: Convenience, price, geography, and large-platform advantages can coexist with anti-scale niches.

### Claim 2

Retail is cultural discovery as well as logistics, so non-scalable local specificity can be valuable.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Retail differentiated by taste, place, and discovery.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-02-06.
- Qualification: Non-scale can also mean higher prices, limited access, fragility, and exclusion.
