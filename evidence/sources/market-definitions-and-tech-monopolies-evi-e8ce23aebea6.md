## Curated excerpt

> *The big shifts in dominance in tech in the last few decades have not generally come from a new product that does the same thing as the old one, but from a company doing something that changes the field of play. Microsoft didn’t overturn IBM’s dominance of mainframes .. Google didn’t make a new Windows, and Facebook didn’t take on Google at web search \- instead, they carved out something new.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Microsoft displaced IBM's mainframe dominance through PCs, Google created search rather than a new Windows, and Facebook created social networking rather than competing directly in search.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Selected historical changes in large technology-company dominance.
- Date relevance: Retrospective cases published 2020-10-31.
- Qualification: The examples are selective summaries and do not isolate all causes of the incumbents' changing positions.

### Claim 2

Major changes in technology dominance often come from changing the field of competition rather than offering a direct substitute within the incumbent's established category.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Technology-market transitions illustrated by IBM, Microsoft, Google, and Facebook.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2020-10-31.
- Qualification: Direct product competition also matters, and a changed field can still depend on incumbent infrastructure, regulation, or complements.
