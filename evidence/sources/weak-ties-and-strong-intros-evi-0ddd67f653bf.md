## Curated excerpt

> *“Suggesting the primacy of structure over motivation” \- \[..\] the structure of the ties, is more important than the motivation of your strong ties. Said another way, while your strong ties are motivated to help you and actively working on your side they also generally know the same people you do, while it’s your weak ties that are likely to be able to connect you to opportunities outside your core network.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Weak ties can connect people to opportunities outside the network shared by their strong ties.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Personal and professional networks.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2020-12-10.
- Qualification: Weak ties do not guarantee a useful introduction.

### Claim 2

Network structure can matter more for opportunity access than the motivation of close contacts.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Opportunity discovery through social networks.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2020-12-10.
- Qualification: Motivation, trust, and tie quality still affect whether a connection helps.
