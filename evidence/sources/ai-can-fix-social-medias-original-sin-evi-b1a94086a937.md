## Curated excerpt

>  *\[AI\] sees a much more complex version of who you are than the old-school social media algorithms that saw you through a keyhole. And this may be its greatest achievement. Technology changes how we see ourselves. In a world where algorithms can only see clicks and watch time, we thought we were a degenerate bunch. Now that algorithms can talk to us, we see a much richer reflection in the mirror.*
