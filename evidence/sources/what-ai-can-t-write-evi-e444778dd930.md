## Curated excerpt

> *We often read to learn. But we also read for reassurance that we’re not alone. That we’re not broken, not bad people, not hopeless. We want to know other people have felt those dark, terrifying feelings we hesitate to name because it might stop anyone from ever loving us again. We want to feel what others have felt. Can a computer reassure you you’re not alone?*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Readers seek writing not only for information but for recognition that other people have shared difficult feelings.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Human functions of personal and literary writing.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-10-24.
- Qualification: Readers also form meaningful responses to fictional, anonymous, collaborative, and machine-assisted texts.

### Claim 2

AI-generated writing may struggle to provide credible human solidarity because the writer has not lived the experience.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Authenticity and reassurance in AI-generated writing.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2022-10-24.
- Qualification: Reader knowledge, disclosure, quality, and human collaboration can alter perceived authenticity.
