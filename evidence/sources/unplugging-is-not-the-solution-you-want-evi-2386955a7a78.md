## Curated excerpt

> *We could be a lot happier, healthier and again productive if we allocated a fraction of our time and energy on conditioning ourselves to new environments and ideas, instead of fervently stressing to avoid them at all costs. This is not tolerance, complacency, minimization or desensitization, but acclimation.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Avoiding technological environments is less adaptive than deliberately acclimating to their conditions.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Individual response to pervasive digital change.
- Date relevance: Position published 2024-09-30.
- Qualification: Withdrawal can still be protective where environments are harmful or coercive.

### Claim 2

People can condition themselves to new environments without endorsing or minimizing their harms.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Psychological and behavioral adaptation.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2024-09-30.
- Qualification: The boundary between acclimation and normalization of harm is difficult to maintain.
