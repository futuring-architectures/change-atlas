- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Consumer online services and prospective metaverse experiences.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: A prospective metaverse spanning separately operated digital services.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-09-28; the dependency is presented within a future-oriented blueprint.
- Qualification: Technical interoperability alone would not ensure commercial cooperation, governance compatibility, or user adoption.
