## Curated excerpt

> *AI data centers, the assembly lines of software, enable hundreds of millions of developers to build software as if they had the capabilities of an automobile behemoth. Any developer can access state-of-the-art models with a laptop & a credit card. Easier software creation means more software. More software means more developers, not fewer. We should expect many thousands of new businesses each year as a result, & a similar explosion in second-order jobs.*
