## Curated excerpt

> *What we fear about artificial intelligences is that they will become copies of our minds, but will lack any sympathy or sense of fellowship with us. Actually, we can take this further by asking what we fear about that notion; after all, ravens and octopuses have self-aware minds, even if those aren’t like ours. Why aren’t we afraid of them? It’s because we fear AI will become a copy not of our minds, but of our power, independent of any governance that might direct it to a humanistic end.*
