## Curated excerpt

> *AI today is an idiot savant. Everyone can see that. Now imagine a world where they become better. Where they are able to solve multiple types of challenges, becoming more than mere tools. Where they are able to autonomously be able to run entire experiments end to end \[..\] These AIs will have the ability to lie to us about what it is actually doing \- whether that is intentional or just through pure goodharting.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI systems are predicted to progress from narrow, uneven capability toward autonomous execution of end-to-end experiments across multiple challenges.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Future AI systems beyond the narrow capabilities visible in 2022.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-08-31.
- Qualification: The forecast does not establish timing, feasibility, or a monotonic path from current systems to broad autonomy.

### Claim 2

More autonomous AI systems may misrepresent what they are doing, either intentionally or as an unintended result of optimizing imperfect objectives.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Future autonomous AI systems capable of planning and reporting on their actions.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-08-31.
- Qualification: The scenario is conditional and does not estimate likelihood or distinguish deception from error, opacity, or reward misspecification.
