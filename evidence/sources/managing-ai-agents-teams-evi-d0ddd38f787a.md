## Curated excerpt

> *We must shift from viewing AI as mere tools to seeing them as collaborators. We need to develop skills in AI coordination, conflict resolution between agents, and strategic task allocation. In essence, we're all becoming managers in the AI era, orchestrating a team of digital assistants to enhance our daily lives and work.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Workers should treat AI agents as collaborators whose use requires coordination, conflict resolution, and task-allocation skills rather than as passive tools alone.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Teams using multiple AI agents for knowledge work.
- Date relevance: Normative position published 2024-12-31.
- Qualification: People remain accountable for agent outputs, and anthropomorphic collaboration language can conceal reliability, security, and power issues.

### Claim 2

As digital assistants proliferate, more workers will perform management-like work by directing and integrating their activities.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Knowledge workers using AI assistants and agent teams.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-12-31.
- Qualification: Management of agents differs from managing people, and automation may also reduce or centralize orchestration rather than distribute it to every worker.
