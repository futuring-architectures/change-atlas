## Curated excerpt

> *The “LLM as individual mind” metaphor has pushed scientific inquiry in certain directions: If you are a scientist operating under that metaphor, it seems perfectly natural to give an LLM an IQ test or any other standardized test designed to evaluate humans \[…\] Such evaluations and inquiries would make much less sense if you conceptualize LLMs as more like sophisticated databases or user-friendly libraries.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The metaphor of an LLM as an individual mind makes human intelligence tests appear like natural evaluation instruments.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: How AI metaphors shape scientific evaluation choices.
- Date relevance: Source published online 2024-11-14.
- Qualification: Researchers can use human tests pragmatically without fully endorsing the individual-mind metaphor.

### Claim 2

Conceptualizing LLMs as databases or libraries would redirect inquiry away from human-style standardized testing.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Counterfactual effect of alternative metaphors on AI research.
- Date relevance: Source published online 2024-11-14.
- Qualification: Different metaphors can coexist and illuminate different system properties; none is a complete model.
