## Curated excerpt

> *Every founder has a data model, whether they realize it or not. Either you choose it explicitly or it gets inherited from whatever you’re copying. Most founders never articulate it. By the time the architecture solidifies around these implicit choices, it’s nearly impossible to change … at the extreme ends of markets—where you’re toppling multi-billion-dollar incumbents or creating entirely new categories—a distinctive data model becomes a critical and non-obvious edge.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1
