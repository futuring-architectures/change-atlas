## Curated excerpt

> *We are just not used to abundant "intelligence" (of a sort), which leads people to miss a huge value of AI. Don't ask for an idea, ask for 30\. Don't ask for a suggestion on how to end a sentence, ask for 20 in different styles. Don't ask for advice, ask for many strategies. Humans curate. And modify. And combine. And reject. And use the AI as inspiration. And know when to avoid using it for inspiration.*
