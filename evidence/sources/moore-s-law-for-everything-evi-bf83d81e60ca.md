## Curated excerpt

> *\[L\]abor is the driving cost at many levels of the supply chain. If robots can build a house on land you already own from natural resources mined and refined onsite, using solar power, the cost of building that house is close to the cost to rent the robots. And if those robots are made by other robots, the cost to rent them will be much less than it was when humans made them.*

### Claim 1

### Claim 2

If robots recursively produce robots and use onsite materials and solar energy, the cost of building a house could approach the cost of renting the robots and fall further as robot production becomes automated.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: A hypothetical highly automated housing supply chain.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-03-16; this is a conditional long-range forecast.
- Qualification: The scenario assumes technical feasibility, access to land and resources, energy sufficiency, and exclusion of regulatory, financing, infrastructure, and distribution costs.
