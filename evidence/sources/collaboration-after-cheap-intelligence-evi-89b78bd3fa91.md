> *When anyone can generate a plan, a spec, a design, a pricing model, a prototype, a pile of code, a launch email, a competitive teardown, a forecast, a hiring rubric and when every employee and every agent can spin up competent output on demand, the constraint stops being “can we think of something?” The constraint becomes: what will we commit to together?*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

When individuals and agents can generate many competent artifacts cheaply, idea production becomes less scarce.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: AI-enabled knowledge work with broad access to capable generation tools.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2026-04-03.
- Qualification: Output competence, context, verification, implementation, and access remain uneven and costly.

### Claim 2

Under cheap intelligence, collective commitment and selection become more binding constraints than the ability to generate options.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Coordination after generative capacity becomes abundant.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2026-04-03.
- Qualification: Some domains remain constrained by expertise, discovery, capital, data, authority, and physical execution rather than commitment alone.
