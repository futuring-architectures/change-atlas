## Curated excerpt

> *\[B\]eing a manager is as much like designing a video game as it is playing a video game. The art of designing a video game is the art of designing context and incentives that enables a player to achieve a goal. That’s all it is \[..\] How do I design an environment in which the team I’m overseeing focuses on the right problem and delivers the right outcome 90% of the time?*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Management can be understood as designing context and incentives that enable people to achieve a goal.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Management as environment design rather than direct task execution.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-10-30.
- Qualification: The game metaphor can understate dignity, politics, intrinsic motivation, ambiguity, and employees' ability to reshape the system.

### Claim 2

Managers should design environments in which teams focus on the right problems and usually produce the intended outcomes.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Team leadership and operating-system design.
- Date relevance: Position published 2022-10-30.
- Qualification: Outcome alignment requires participation and feedback; incentives can be gamed and managerial definitions of the right problem can be wrong.
