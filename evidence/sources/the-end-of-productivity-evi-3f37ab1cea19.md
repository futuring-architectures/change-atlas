## Curated excerpt

> *Productivity tools shape our thinking in ways that favor standardization, efficiency, and predictability. They demand structure before inspiration has a chance to strike. They ask for timelines when the problem itself is still hazy \[…\] But for creative information, what you need is tools that encourage connections, facilitate serendipity, and support non-linear thinking.*
