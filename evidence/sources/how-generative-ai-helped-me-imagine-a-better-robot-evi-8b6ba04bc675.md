## Curated excerpt

> *Co-designing with AI was an illuminating experience. A prompt can cover many attributes, including the subject, medium, environment, color, and even mood. A good prompt, I learned, needed to be specific because I wanted the design to serve a particular purpose. On the other hand, I wanted to be surprised by the results. I discovered that I needed to strike a balance between what I knew and wanted, and what I didn’t know or couldn’t imagine but might want.*
