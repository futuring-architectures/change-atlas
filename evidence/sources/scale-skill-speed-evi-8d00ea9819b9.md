## Curated excerpt

> *Not only can individuals now work at the scale of companies by upskilling, unleashing their talent, scaling through their teams, and building reputations as stellar as big firms but large companies now need to re-configure around talent rather than believing talent will cluster around companies. The shift of power from big to small, management to talent, and from the towers of power to the talent with wrenches in the trenches has only just begun.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Digital platforms and tools allow individuals and small organizations to operate at scales previously associated with large firms, requiring large firms to reconfigure around talent.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Knowledge work and media-oriented organizations using modern platforms and AI tools.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2023-03-19.
- Qualification: Access to scalable tools does not remove capital, distribution, reputation, regulation, or coordination advantages held by large firms.

### Claim 2

Power will shift from large organizations toward smaller actors and from management toward distinctive talent.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Organizational power in technology-enabled knowledge work.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2023-03-19.
- Qualification: Platforms can simultaneously empower individuals and concentrate power in infrastructure owners, customers, and capital providers.
