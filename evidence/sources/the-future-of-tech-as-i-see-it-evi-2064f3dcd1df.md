## Curated excerpt

> *\[T\]echnology only moves in one direction. There is no going back. It is not as if Zoom lost its value along with valuations. Who wouldn’t rather talk to a screen than fly five hours for a meeting? No matter how much we want to use the past as a reference point for the future, we have to override our biases and go where the value takes us. And where it takes us isn’t always where we might think \[..\] We have to consider what we know about the foundations of tech.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Once a technology creates durable practical value, a decline in financial valuation does not erase that utility.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Adopted digital technologies whose companies or markets experience valuation declines.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-07-21 and uses Zoom as an example.
- Qualification: Use value can persist while adoption falls or alternatives emerge; the excerpt does not establish irreversibility for every technology.

### Claim 2

Interpreting technology’s future requires overriding backward-looking bias and following underlying value and technical foundations.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Technology forecasting and investment interpretation.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-07-21.
- Qualification: The recommendation does not provide a method for distinguishing durable value from temporary adoption or hype.
