## Curated excerpt

> *For AI, a science experiment, there’s nothing, nothing, nothing… then BOOM. A fully formed butterfly emerges from the chrysalis. For web3, a social experiment, little flints spark adoption, and adoption shapes the products. Nothing, something, more, more, “probably nothing”… then KABOOM. Something unexpected breaks, and it’s back to the drawing board.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Science experiments can show abrupt capability breakthroughs after long invisible development.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Comparison of technical research with social systems.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-10-10.
- Qualification: Scientific progress often also accumulates visibly and iteratively.

### Claim 2

Social experiments evolve through adoption feedback and can break unexpectedly after apparent growth.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Web3 and other adoption-dependent systems.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-10-10.
- Qualification: The binary between science and social experiments is heuristic rather than exhaustive.
