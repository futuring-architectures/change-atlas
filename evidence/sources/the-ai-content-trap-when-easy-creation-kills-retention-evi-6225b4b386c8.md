## Curated excerpt

> *If you want to increase the quality of content, you need to increase creative friction in the content creation process, i.e. introduce constraints or make it harder to create, not easier…The users that do create will put more thought and care into their creations. As a result, the output will generate more interest from the consumption side of the network leading to increased retention.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Reducing creative friction can lower content quality and weaken consumer retention in a content network.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: AI-enabled user-generated content products.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2026-06-01.
- Qualification: Friction can also suppress participation, and quality effects depend on moderation and audience goals.

### Claim 2

Introducing constraints can increase creator care and thereby make output more interesting to consumers.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Network products with cheap generative creation.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2026-06-01.
- Qualification: The mechanism needs controlled product and retention evidence.
