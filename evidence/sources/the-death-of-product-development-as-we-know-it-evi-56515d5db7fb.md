## Curated excerpt

> *Traditional product development has been \*best practice-ized\* until it resembles an Ikea instruction manual \[…\] This worked when software moved in cycles of months. But now that we have AI at our disposal (who is today an insanely productive, cheap yet junior talent that must be aggressively micromanaged), that tidy linear path is going the way of the dinosaurs.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

A linear sequence of ideas, polished mockups, requirements, code, and launch is mismatched to AI-enabled product development that can test many options quickly.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Conventional software product-development practices under generative-AI acceleration.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2025-03-25.
- Qualification: Regulated, safety-critical, interdependent, or large-scale products may still require staged design, specification, verification, and coordination.

### Claim 2

Current AI can act like a highly productive and inexpensive junior contributor that requires close direction and evaluation.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Operational metaphor for AI-assisted product development in early 2025.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2025-03-25.
- Qualification: AI is not an employee and lacks responsibility, durable context, and human learning; costs and reliability vary across tools and tasks.
