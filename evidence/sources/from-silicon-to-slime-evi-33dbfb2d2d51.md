## Curated excerpt

> *At some point I think we’ll fully realize that what’s happening in the natural world is more computationally efficient, inventive, and resilient than anything we could create from silicon. So then the question becomes: what can we do with those capacities? Can biology teach us how to create new computing architectures that aren’t so extractive? We’re literally running out of sand. What’s the new substrate going to be? Could it be alive?*
