## Curated excerpt

> *The internet is the ultimate variance-amplifying institution. It inverts many of the core features of the \[institutions\] that preceded it. Above all, it is permissionless and uncapped. On the internet anyone can do anything and, in theory, can reach any scale ... the internet does not select for the least disruptive or the most conforming, but for the most meme-able, share-able and bandwagon-able.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The internet is a variance-amplifying institution because it permits participation without prior permission and allows outcomes to scale without a fixed ceiling.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Institutional effects of the open internet around early 2021.
- Date relevance: Source publication recovered to month precision (2021-01).
- Qualification: Permission and scale still vary by platform, infrastructure access, law, and moderation.

### Claim 2

Compared with hierarchical institutions, the internet selects for behavior that is memeable, shareable, and capable of triggering bandwagons rather than behavior that is conforming or minimally disruptive.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Selection effects of internet-mediated institutions.
- Date relevance: Source publication recovered to month precision (2021-01).
- Qualification: The contrast is ideal-typical; many online systems also enforce conformity and many offline institutions amplify variance.
