## Curated excerpt

> *\[T\]here is no actual precedent for saying that scraping data to train an AI is fair use; all of these companies are relying on ancient internet cases that allowed search engines and social media platforms to exist in the first place. It’s messy, and it feels like all of those decisions are up for grabs in what promises to be a decade of litigation. So now imagine that you are Google, which on the one hand operates YouTube, and on the other hand is racing to build generative AI products \[…\]*
