## Curated excerpt

> *Once an agent is cast as a co-worker, the unit of adoption becomes the role, not the workflow or the system in which that workflow is embedded \[…\] We need to stop taking the digital labor metaphor literally to see the questions that truly matter: Which agentic capabilities must be proprietary? How should workflows be restructured around them? What sensing mechanisms reveal how capability values are shifting? How should humans be repositioned as the boundary evolves?*
