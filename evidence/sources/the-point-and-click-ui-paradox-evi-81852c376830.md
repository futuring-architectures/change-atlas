## Curated excerpt

> *\[…\] as our graphical interfaces grew bloated and overwhelming, clicking became the friction \[…\] For actions below your personal complexity threshold, buttons will reign supreme. But for complex, multi-layered tasks, the era of hunting through endless menus is ending. Tomorrow’s best software won’t force you to choose between clicking and typing. Instead, it will seamlessly learn your habits and offer you the exact right tool for the job.*
