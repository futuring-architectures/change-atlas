## Curated excerpt

> *When an agent can generate a complete version of a feature in minutes, you don’t need to specify every detail in advance. You provide the direction, the agent builds a version, you look at it, you adjust, you try a different approach. You can generate ten versions and pick the best one. Requirements aren’t a phase anymore. They’re a byproduct of iteration.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Fast agent-generated prototypes can turn requirements from a separate preliminary phase into outputs of iterative evaluation.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: AI-assisted product and software development.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2026-02-20.
- Qualification: High-stakes and regulated work may retain substantial up-front specification.

### Claim 2

When generation is cheap, teams can explore multiple implementations and select among them instead of specifying one in detail first.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Iterative software design with generative agents.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2026-02-20.
- Qualification: Evaluation capacity becomes a critical constraint.
