## Curated excerpt

> *At its core the technium is an ecosystem of inventions capable of evolving entirely new forms of being that wet biology alone can not reach. Our technologies are ultimately not contrary to life, but are in fact an extension of life, enabling it to develop yet more options and possibilities at a faster rate. Increasing options and possibilities is also known as progress, so in the end, what the technium brings us humans is progress.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The technium is an ecosystem of interdependent inventions that can evolve possibilities beyond those available to biological evolution alone.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Kevin Kelly's technium concept as stated in the 2023 interview.
- Date relevance: Source publication verified (2023-03-07).
- Qualification: The ecosystem and evolution language is an analogy and does not establish organism-level agency or independent goals.

### Claim 2

Technology extends life by increasing options and possibilities more rapidly, and this increase constitutes progress.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Kevin Kelly's normative interpretation of technological possibility and progress.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2023-03-07.
- Qualification: More options can carry unequal access, irreversible harms, coordination costs, and choices that do not improve well-being.
