## Curated excerpt

> *The best part is there’s no multiplayer in Claude Code yet. I get to disappear and just have explore again. I get to actually stay focused instead of performing focus. What most companies call “collaboration” is actually performative proximity. Everyone in the same files, the same channels, always visible, always accessible. And the output isn’t special. It’s a beige mean. Everything smoothed down to whatever idea survived the panopticon.*
