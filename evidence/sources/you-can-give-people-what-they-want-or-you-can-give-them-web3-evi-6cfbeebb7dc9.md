## Curated excerpt

> *If you’re a VC, financialization might seem natural and even fun. But most normal people don’t find financialization fun in the least \[..\] The idea that they’d want to get into a complicated financial transaction, with a nuanced risk/reward calculus, to play a game or listen to a podcast, probably sounds fun to a few galaxy-brained VCs—and pretty much no one else.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Most people do not experience financialization as enjoyable in the way venture investors may.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Mainstream consumers in contrast with venture-capital participants evaluating Web3 products.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-06-28.
- Qualification: The excerpt makes a broad audience judgment without presenting consumer research or distinguishing different financial products.

### Claim 2

Requiring a complex financial transaction and risk-reward calculation to access entertainment creates a mainstream-adoption barrier.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Web3 games, podcasts, and other consumer experiences that require financial participation.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-06-28.
- Qualification: The barrier depends on product design, perceived value, risk, and audience; some users may actively prefer financial participation.
