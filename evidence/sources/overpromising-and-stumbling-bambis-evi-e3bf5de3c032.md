## Curated excerpt

> *If I had to choose, I’d prefer to be part of a culture which encourages exploration and experimentation, but that will require a change in our collective patience. Flaws are part of every new thing, and we need to remember that. If we’re to make progress we’ll need to give these ideas time to marinate and evolve, the third generation of this ‘new thing’ might well become a vital part of our everyday lives if we don’t kill it first.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

A culture of exploration and experimentation requires greater collective patience with the flaws of new technologies and products.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Public and organizational responses to early-stage technology.

### Claim 2

An imperfect first generation of a new technology may evolve into a vital third generation if early flaws do not cause the idea to be abandoned prematurely.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: The maturation of novel technologies and products.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-02-14.
- Qualification: Many new technologies do not improve enough to become valuable, so the possibility does not justify every continuation.
