## Curated excerpt

> *Long-tail users of user-centered design are not given the degree of control necessary to adapt the design object or tool to their unique needs \[..\] In design for emergence, the designer assumes that the end-user holds relevant knowledge and gives them extensive control over the design. Rather than designing the end result, we design the user’s experience of designing their own end result.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Design for emergence gives users substantial control to adapt a tool using knowledge that designers do not possess.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Tools serving heterogeneous or long-tail needs.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-06-08.
- Qualification: User control can conflict with safety, coherence, and supportability.

### Claim 2

Designers can design the experience through which users create their own outcomes instead of specifying the final outcome.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Participatory and adaptable system design.
- Date relevance: Position published 2022-06-08.
- Qualification: The excerpt does not identify when this approach is inappropriate.
