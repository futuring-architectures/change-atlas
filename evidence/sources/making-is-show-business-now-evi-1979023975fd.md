## Curated excerpt

> *Today, the natural constraint on open-source projects has become like an upside-down Tragedy of the Commons problem \[..\] Here, the commons at risk of being overdrawn isn’t the product; the code itself can be consumed limitlessly. The risk is that the creators become overdrawn and overwhelmed with participation, and with demands on their time.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Open-source code can be consumed without depletion while maintainer attention is depleted by participation and support demands.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Open-source projects with a small maintainer core and a large user or contributor audience.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2020-10-08.
- Qualification: The balance varies by project modularity, governance, funding, documentation, and contributor capability.

### Claim 2

Successful creator platforms must manage attention so that audience participation sustains rather than exhausts creators.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Creator and open-source platforms where success increases interaction and maintenance work.
- Date relevance: Position published 2020-10-08.
- Qualification: Boundary-setting can limit access, and platform tools alone cannot resolve funding, power, or care obligations.
