## Curated excerpt

> *Software may be eating the world, but looking around the AI landscape the buffet is starting by very much trying to eat its own. The people who build software are busy wiring up the machines that will build software in their place. Call it automation. Call it augmentation. Call it whatever makes you sleep. What it actually is, is software industrialization, happening right in front of our eyes.*
