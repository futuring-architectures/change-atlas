## Curated excerpt

> *When technology reduces the cost of production and distribution, production shifts away from singular, visionary efforts to collective, decentralized efforts \[…\] Production relies increasingly less on an isolated process (eg, a novelist making many choices to their own story) and turns it into a continuous and constant conversation with the network. It is collective cultural production: many choices on the edge producing culture together.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1
