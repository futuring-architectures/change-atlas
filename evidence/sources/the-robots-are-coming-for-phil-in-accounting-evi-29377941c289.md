## Curated excerpt

> *This shift may be related to the popularity of “so-so automation” — technology that is just barely good enough to replace human workers, but not good enough to create new jobs or make companies significantly more productive. This simple, substitutive kind of automation threatens not just individual workers, but the economy as a whole.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

“So-so automation” is technology just capable enough to replace workers but not capable enough to create new jobs or substantially raise productivity.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Automation that substitutes for human tasks without a corresponding productivity transformation.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-03-06.
- Qualification: The category is a diagnostic framing; the excerpt does not provide a threshold for “significant” productivity or job creation.

### Claim 2

Substitutive automation can threaten both displaced workers and wider economic performance.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Economies adopting so-so automation at meaningful scale.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-03-06.
- Qualification: The excerpt states the systemic risk without estimating displacement, productivity, wages, demand, or compensating job creation.
