## Curated excerpt

> *We’re entering strange territory. For the first time, the constraint won’t be what’s technically possible. It’ll be what’s humanly manageable. The companies that master this will build products that feel simultaneously simple and infinitely capable. The ones that don’t will build everything and achieve nothing. What happens when you can build everything? You learn that building was never really the hard part.*
