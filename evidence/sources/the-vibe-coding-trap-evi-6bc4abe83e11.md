## Curated excerpt

> *None of this means that the SaaS industry isn’t going to change. Something is happening, but it’s not the death of SaaS. It’s the death of bad SaaS. For years, you could build a mediocre product in an uncrowded category and charge for it simply because the cost of building an alternative was high. Those days are gone. If your product is a thin wrapper around a CRUD database with a nice UI, you are indeed in trouble.*
