## Curated excerpt

> *Ultimately, though, current LLM tech faces a fundamental roadblock when it comes to resolving ambiguity: it has no durable internal mental model of you as a writer or interlocutor. An LLM can’t (yet) see you, or look at your profile picture, or creep on your socials or LinkedIn bio, so it can’t do what we humans do and think to itself, “What might this person with these qualities have meant or intended by this turn of phrase?”*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Current LLMs lack a durable internal model of a specific interlocutor.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Capabilities of GPT-4-era language models in sustained interpersonal context.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-05-18.
- Qualification: The claim concerns the model and product context available at publication; later systems may add persistent user models externally.

### Claim 2

Without contextual knowledge of the speaker, an LLM is limited in resolving ambiguous language the way humans use social cues to infer intent.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Language understanding under interpersonal ambiguity.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-05-18.
- Qualification: Humans also misread intent, and external profiles or memory can partly compensate without constituting human-like understanding.
