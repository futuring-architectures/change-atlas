## Curated excerpt

> *\[M\]y main worry with AI is that people are too focused on the technology in isolation rather than seeing it as a platform, like electricity or the mobile phone, that will have much broader ramifications. To use some more econ jargon, humans tend to think in terms of the comparative statics, in which one thing changes while everything is held constant, rather than in terms of general equilibrium.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI should be analyzed as a general-purpose platform with broad second- and third-order effects, not only as an isolated technology.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Economic and strategic analysis of AI adoption.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-12-11.
- Qualification: The platform analogy does not establish which effects will dominate or on what timescale.

### Claim 2

Static analysis that changes one variable while holding the rest constant is inadequate for anticipating AI’s economy-wide ramifications.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: General-equilibrium analysis of AI.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-12-11.
- Qualification: General-equilibrium reasoning is directionally broader but remains sensitive to model assumptions.
