## Curated excerpt

> *All systems are fictions that we project onto our environment for our own instrumental purposes. Those purposes are determined by our mindset, by the way we see the world around us. If the pursuit of our instrumental goals results in catastrophe, perhaps it is possible to change our mindset, the mindset or paradigm out of which the system — its goals, structure, rules, delays, parameters — arises?*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Systems are instrumental models projected onto environments rather than neutral, self-evident objects.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Systems thinking and model construction.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-11-18.
- Qualification: Material structures and causal constraints exist even when system boundaries and purposes are constructed.

### Claim 2

When a system’s goals produce catastrophe, changing the originating mindset or paradigm can alter its goals, rules, structure, delays, and parameters.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: High-leverage intervention in harmful systems.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-11-18.
- Qualification: Paradigm change may be difficult, contested, slow, or insufficient without material and institutional changes.
