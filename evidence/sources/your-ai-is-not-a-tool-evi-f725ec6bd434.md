## Curated excerpt

> *Your AI is not a tool. It is an environment, and you are in it \[…\] it is an environment which envelops the user and works on us from the inside out while we naively think that we remain unchanged by our use so long as we are using it carefully and intentionally. The care and intentionality is beside the point, and our confidence in such vigilance probably works against us in the long run.*
