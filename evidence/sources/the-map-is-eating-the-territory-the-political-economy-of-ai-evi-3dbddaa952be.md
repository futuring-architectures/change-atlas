## Curated excerpt

> *If you want LLMs to have long term value, you need to have an accompanying social system in which humans keep on producing the knowledge, the art and the information that makes them valuable. Intellectual property systems without incentives for the production of valuable human knowledge will render LLMs increasingly worthless over time.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

LLM value depends on a continuing social system that produces human knowledge, art, and information.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Political economy of training and maintaining generative models.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2024-02-12.
- Qualification: Models can also use synthetic data and private feedback, but those sources retain grounding and quality constraints.

### Claim 2

Intellectual-property arrangements that remove incentives for valuable human production can degrade the long-run value of LLMs.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Effects of knowledge-production incentives on model ecosystems.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-02-12.
- Qualification: The relationship depends on compensation models, open production, public funding, licensing, and technical change.
