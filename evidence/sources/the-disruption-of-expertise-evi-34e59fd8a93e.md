## Curated excerpt

> *The New Expert, empowered by the internet, has a nearly infallible editor at her fingertips in the form of Search and sprawling Social Networks. The New Expert can be transformed into a polymath after the fashion of Newton, Locke, Rousseau, et al., as a consequence of both now having no upper-bounds to what she can learn, and having audiences hungry for the same infinite knowledge-horizons made available to anyone with wi-fi ..*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Search and social networks give internet-enabled learners continuous editorial feedback and access to broad fields of knowledge.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Self-directed expertise formation online.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-10-08.
- Qualification: Information abundance does not ensure accuracy, synthesis, or deep competence.

### Claim 2

Internet access can enable a new kind of polymath whose learning is not bounded by a single professional field.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Knowledge workers and independent learners with network access.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2021-10-08.
- Qualification: The claim may overstate access equality and understate time, mentorship, and credential constraints.
