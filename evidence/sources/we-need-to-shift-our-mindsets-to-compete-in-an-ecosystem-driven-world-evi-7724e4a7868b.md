## Curated excerpt

> *Competitive advantage can no longer be reduced to the sum of efficiencies in a value chain, but is embedded in webs of connections. To compete in an ecosystem-driven world, leaders need to do more than adapt how we deploy assets, we need to look at things differently. It is no longer enough to merely plan and direct action, we need to inspire and empower belief \[..\] Today your fate depends less on the assets and capabilities you control, than what you can access.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

In an ecosystem model, competitive advantage depends on webs of connection and on capabilities a firm can access, not only on assets it controls or efficiencies within its value chain.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Competitive strategy in the ecosystem-driven model proposed by the author.
- Date relevance: Source publication verified (2022-07-02).
- Qualification: Control, internal capabilities, and value-chain efficiency can remain decisive even when external access matters.

### Claim 2

Leaders in ecosystem-driven settings should inspire and empower shared belief rather than rely only on planning, direction, and asset deployment.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Leadership of organizations dependent on external ecosystems.
- Date relevance: Normative proposal published 2022-07-02.
- Qualification: Empowerment and belief do not replace contracts, accountability, operational coordination, or conflict resolution.
