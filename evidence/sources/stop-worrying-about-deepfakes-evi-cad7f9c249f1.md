## Curated excerpt

> *One thing I came to appreciate more \[…\] is the value of parody and satire in human communication. This is a very old format for making a social critique, often used quite strategically \[…\] A lot of the internet is just like that. It’s awash in transgressive material that requires you to think a bit to get the message. The problem is, the feed-like nature of social media often causes us to stick to the surface-level message, so then you get these outcries.*
