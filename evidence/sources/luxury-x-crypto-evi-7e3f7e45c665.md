## Curated excerpt

> *Luxury has always been in the business of simulation: of making its stories believable by the massive numbers of people. Coco Chanel was an artist; Louis Vuitton was a craftsman; Hermès has inimitable heritage; Ralph Lauren is about American aristocracy; Möet Hennessy has been made according to a secret recipe transmitted over generations.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Luxury brands and symbolic value.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-01-10.
- Qualification: The formulation foregrounds narrative while omitting material quality, scarcity, distribution, and price as other sources of value.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Narrative strategies used by established luxury brands.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-01-10.
- Qualification: The examples illustrate a repertoire but do not show that every luxury brand relies on the same archetypes.
