## Curated excerpt

> *For product owners and feature team product managers, where the job is primarily about delivery, and driven by a delivery process, it seems pretty clear to me that there will be very substantial productivity improvements, if not full automation \[…\] Most jobs involve some blend of judgement and process, and the process aspects are prime targets for the tools. What remains is the judgement.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Generative AI will substantially automate process-heavy product-delivery work.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Product-management, design, engineering, and delivery roles described by the author.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2025-02-25.
- Qualification: Automation potential differs by task and context and does not establish adoption speed, quality, accountability, or net employment effects.

### Claim 2

Work that is primarily process is more automatable than work requiring contextual judgment, which remains a human contribution.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: The process-versus-judgment boundary in product work.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2025-02-25.
- Qualification: Process and judgment are intertwined, models can support some judgments, and organizations may codify formerly tacit judgment over time.
