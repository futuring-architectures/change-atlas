## Curated excerpt

> *To succeed in an age where data is plentiful, pattern matching cheap, and machines are becoming smarter \[…\] it will be key to ask ourselves all the time what we offer as a) a perspective, b) a point of view, c) a provocation and/or d) a plan of action on everything we have imbibed versus just reporting facts and process. If we do, we will forever grow and succeed. If we do not, we will be undifferentiated and will be automated away…*
