## Curated excerpt

> *While most of us assume corporations (e.g., big tech) as existing today, the idea of a corporation was new post-war. Not simply the legal definition, but the very nature of how work was done. Perhaps surprising, but the answer to future of work was once: “The Corporation” \[..\] The question is not “work remotely”, but what is the very structure of getting things done?*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The postwar corporation was itself an organizational innovation shaped by its historical conditions.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: The development of the large modern corporation after World War II.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2021-06-12.
- Qualification: Corporations predate the postwar era; the claim concerns the dominant operating form, not legal invention.

### Claim 2

The future-of-work question concerns the structure for getting things done, not only where employees work.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Organizational redesign beyond remote-versus-office location.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-06-12.
- Qualification: Location remains materially important for access, care, real estate, geography, and tasks requiring physical presence.
