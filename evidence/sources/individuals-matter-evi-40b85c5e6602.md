## Curated excerpt

> *One way to address this problem \[of complexity in a company with thousands of engineers\] is by reducing the perceived complexity of the problem via imagining that individuals are fungible, making the system more legible…it's highly scalable, and if there's one thing that tech companies like, it's doing things that scale, and treating a complex system like it's SimCity or Civilization is highly scalable.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Treating individual workers as fungible is a legibility simplification that makes large organizations easier to plan and administer.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Headcount planning, staffing, compensation, and roadmap administration in complex organizations.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-11-15.
- Qualification: Some abstraction is necessary for planning; the problem is relying on it where individual or team effects are material.

### Claim 2

When effectiveness is heavy-tailed, scalable people abstractions can destroy substantial value by ignoring individual capability, fit, and team composition.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Organizations in which individual or team contributions vary widely.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2021-11-15.
- Qualification: The magnitude and distribution of individual effects vary across tasks, and exceptional-case anecdotes do not by themselves estimate organization-wide returns.
