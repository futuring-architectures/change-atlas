## Curated excerpt

> *LLMs are architected around one person, one model, one history \[…\] there is no shared surface that multiple people can gather around simultaneously and differently. The interesting design question the metaphor raises is: what would it mean to build a genuinely shared AI conversation? \[…\] one where different people bring different perspectives to the same exchange, and the AI holds the space between them rather than collapsing into a single interlocutor?*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Single-user LLM architecture lacks a shared surface on which multiple people can hold different perspectives simultaneously.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Collaborative AI interface design.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2026-06-03.
- Qualification: Multi-user tools and shared documents can partially supply such surfaces.

### Claim 2

Shared AI should hold space among participants rather than collapse discussion into one user-model history.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Design of collaborative AI systems.
- Date relevance: Position published 2026-06-03.
- Qualification: Shared context raises privacy, power, moderation, and representation challenges.
