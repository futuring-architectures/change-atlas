## Curated excerpt

> *Think of the way computer languages evolved to create more abstraction layers between the user and the underlying code operations, so that we could do more with less. Extrapolate that trend line out towards natural language controllers and you can imagine a programmable organisation where you can spin up a team space, apps and design and build the processes needed to operate, simply by describing what you want.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Natural-language control could become a higher abstraction layer through which people describe and instantiate programmable organizations.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Future organizational systems combining AI, workflows, and software services.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-01-23.
- Qualification: Natural-language interfaces do not by themselves provide reliable execution, governance, shared context, or accountability.

### Claim 2

Successive abstraction layers reduce the distance between human intent and executable organizational operations.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: The source's analogy between programming-language abstraction and AI-mediated organization design.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2024-01-23.
- Qualification: Abstraction can conceal failure modes and shift complexity into evaluation, integration, and exception handling rather than eliminate it.
