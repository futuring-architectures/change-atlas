## Curated excerpt

> *\[The\] supply chain implicates a high-volume aggregated-then-distributed subscription commerce service needing to make multinational pay-ins and pay-outs \[..\] And the capability to offer this allowed entrepreneurs to create a business model which looks very little like existing firms; it is neither vertically integrated nor does it recapitulate the supply chain for most physical items with embedded IP.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

A fantasy-miniature supply chain combined high-volume subscription aggregation with multinational payments to many producers and service providers.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: The 3D-printed fantasy-miniature ecosystem documented in the source.
- Date relevance: Source publication verified (2022-04-08).
- Qualification: The case is a detailed industry example, not evidence that this payment pattern is prevalent across supply chains.

### Claim 2

Software and payment capabilities enabled a business model that was neither vertically integrated nor organized like a conventional physical-goods supply chain with embedded intellectual property.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Business-model effects of payments and software in the documented miniature ecosystem.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2022-04-08.
- Qualification: The ecosystem still contains vertically integrated components and depends on marketplaces, hardware vendors, logistics, and legal IP arrangements.
