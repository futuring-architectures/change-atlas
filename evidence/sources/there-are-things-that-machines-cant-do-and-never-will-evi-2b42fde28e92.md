## Curated excerpt

> *Once we stop imagining AI to be a super genius in a box and begin to see it as a machine for curating infinite monkeys, something much more valuable begins to emerge—a tool to create dialogues for ourselves. Large language models, by definition, hold a multitude of perspectives. So rather than replacing us, we can use them as soundings board to help us create for ourselves.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Language models can serve as sounding boards by presenting multiple learned perspectives for a person to evaluate.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Reflective and creative use of LLMs.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2024-05-12.
- Qualification: Model perspectives inherit training-data biases and are not independent minds.

AI is more valuable as a dialogue partner for human creation than as an imagined super-genius replacement.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Human-machine creative practice.
- Date relevance: Position published 2024-05-12.
- Qualification: Some tasks benefit from automation without dialogue, and dialogue still requires verification.
