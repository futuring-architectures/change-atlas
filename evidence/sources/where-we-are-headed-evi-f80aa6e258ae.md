## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI-enabled firms will become more top-heavy, variable, and adaptive over the next decade, at times appearing almost biological in their responsiveness.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Medium-term organizational effects of capable AI agents.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2025-03-27.
- Qualification: The direction depends on agent capability, reliability, governance, labor institutions, and whether control centralizes or distributes.

### Claim 2

The AI-enabled firm is a proposed new organizational form in which large populations of agents expand intellectual capacity and managerial control.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: The author's speculative model of firms adopting advanced agents.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2025-03-27.
- Qualification: The form is prospective, and the biological metaphor should not be mistaken for agency, personhood, or demonstrated organizational performance.
