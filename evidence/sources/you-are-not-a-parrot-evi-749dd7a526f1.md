## Curated excerpt

> *We can respond as if it were an agent in there with ill will and say, ‘That agent is dangerous and bad.’ That’s the Terminator fantasy version of this, right?” \[..\] Then there’s option two: We could say, ‘Hey, look, this is technology that really encourages people to interpret it as if there were an agent in there with ideas and thoughts and credibility and stuff like that.’ Why is the tech designed like this? Why try to make users believe the bot has intention, that it’s like us?*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Conversational AI is designed in ways that encourage users to infer an intentional, credible agent.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Chatbot interaction design.

- Qualification: Users differ in anthropomorphism and can understand the system while engaging socially.

### Claim 2

AI risk should include how interface design induces false beliefs about agency, not only fantasies of hostile machine intention.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Public understanding and design of conversational AI.
- Date relevance: Position published 2023-03-01.
- Qualification: Agency framing may also support usability if limitations remain legible.
