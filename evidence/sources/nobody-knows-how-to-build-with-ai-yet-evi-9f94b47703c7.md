## Curated excerpt

> *I wasn't programming. I wasn't not-programming either. I don’t know what to call it. We don’t have the word for it yet. The skill isn't syntax anymore. It's not algorithms. It's not even system design, really. It's something like "coherent desire" or "precise imagination" or "structured wishing"…We're all junior developers again…in a permanent sense, where the technology evolves faster than expertise can accumulate.*
