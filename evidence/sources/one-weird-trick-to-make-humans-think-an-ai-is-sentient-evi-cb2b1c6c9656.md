## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Convincing conversational output from a large language model is not evidence that the system is conscious.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Large language models evaluated through conversational interaction.

- Qualification: The excerpt rejects one inference from behavior; it does not supply a general test for consciousness.

### Claim 2

Large language models produce conversational responses by matching prompts to statistically suitable continuations learned from large text corpora.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Autoregressive large language models available in 2022.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-06-13.
- Qualification: The autocomplete analogy is simplifying and does not fully describe representation, training, inference, or later model architectures.
