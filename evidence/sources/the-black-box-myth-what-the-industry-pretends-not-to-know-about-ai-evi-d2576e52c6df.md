## Curated excerpt

> *The shared myth-making around the black box of AI cultivates useful confusion about what can and cannot be known. It helps to cultivate the systems as more mysterious, even sublime, than they really are. It leads to a cottage industry of thinkers…This helps sell them, but also drives fears of doom that can redirect concerns from real people to hypothetical systems and abstract future scenarios.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Treating AI as an unknowable black box cultivates useful confusion and makes systems appear more mysterious and sublime than they are.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Industry and public discourse about AI interpretability.
- Date relevance: Source published 2025-06-17.
- Qualification: Some system behavior is genuinely difficult to explain even though uncertainty can also be strategically exaggerated.

### Claim 2

The black-box myth both helps sell AI and amplifies doom narratives that redirect attention from real people and present harms toward hypothetical systems and abstract futures.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Attention effects of AI mystification.
- Date relevance: Source published 2025-06-17.
- Qualification: Long-term risk analysis and attention to present human impacts are not necessarily mutually exclusive.
