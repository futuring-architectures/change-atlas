## Curated excerpt

> *\[W\]orkslop uniquely uses machines to offload cognitive work to another human being. When coworkers receive workslop, they are often required to take on the burden of decoding the content, inferring missed or false context. A cascade of effortful and complex decision-making processes may follow, including rework and uncomfortable exchanges with colleagues.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Recipients of low-quality AI-generated workplace output reported having to decode intent, reconstruct context, verify content, and redo the work.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: The workplace 'workslop' cases and survey responses reported by the authors in 2025.
- Date relevance: Contemporaneous study report published 2025-09-22.
- Qualification: The excerpt does not provide the sample design or prevalence estimate; reported experiences may not generalize across organizations or uses.

### Claim 2

AI use can shift cognitive labor from the producer of an output to colleagues who must interpret and repair it rather than remove labor from the system.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Organizational productivity effects of low-quality AI-assisted work products.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2025-09-22.
- Qualification: High-quality use can reduce total effort; the net effect depends on task design, review, incentives, and recipient needs.
