## Curated excerpt

> *With innovation, variance generation is not spontaneous, as it is in the natural world. It must be instigated by people looking for new ways to do things or seeking new understanding. But most of the time, innovation teams limit their thinking to tweaks and refinements of ideas that are known to work \[..\] Breakthrough innovation requires contemplation of alternatives beyond current scientific, technological, design, or economic horizons.*
