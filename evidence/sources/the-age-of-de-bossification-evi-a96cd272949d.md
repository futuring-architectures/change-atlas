## Curated excerpt

> *The unbundling and distribution of where and when we work has just begun and culture, teams and excellence will need to be cultivated across time and space with limited in-person interaction as the age of AI, Web 3, XR will make working anywhere and everywhere increasingly the default state in white collar industries making old-fashioned monitoring, controlling, and overseeing difficult.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Distributed work combined with AI, Web3, and extended-reality tools will make working across locations and times increasingly normal in white-collar industries.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: White-collar work organization.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-04-21.
- Qualification: Adoption varies by occupation, regulation, culture, infrastructure, and employer strategy; several bundled technologies may contribute differently.

### Claim 2

Organizations should cultivate culture, teams, and excellence through leadership and influence rather than depend on monitoring, control, and physical oversight.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Leadership in distributed and unbundled work.
- Date relevance: Normative position published 2024-04-21.
- Qualification: Influence and coaching do not remove the need for authority, accountability, performance management, or worker protections.
