## Curated excerpt

> *Without a large industry and the promise of scale, it is hard to justify investment in digitizing an entire process. The world is too complex\! The high upfront costs contribute to slow market share growth. Each incremental customer requires increasing amounts of effort to build the service they need \[..\] Remember, we need 1s and 0s accuracy\! Current software reduces the costs within firms more than it reduces the cost of market transactions.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Digitizing an operational process requires high upfront investment because software must encode detailed instructions and accommodate heterogeneous real-world processes.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: The cost structure of process digitization in firms.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2021-08-10.
- Qualification: Modern machine learning and configurable platforms can reduce some specification costs, but they do not eliminate integration, exception, and change-management work.

### Claim 2

Current software often lowers coordination costs inside firms more than it lowers the cost of conducting the same transactions through markets.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Software's effect on the transaction-cost boundary of the firm.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-08-10.
- Qualification: The balance varies by industry, modularity, standards, contracting infrastructure, and the maturity of external platforms.
