## Curated excerpt

> *Organisations don't fail at innovation because they're bad at the 'innovating' bit. That is, taking an idea and building out a product/service/business concept (though there’s still a lot of room for improvement in designing viable businesses). Instead they're bad at the bit around that: managing the investment in innovation.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Organizations can execute idea-to-product development while still managing innovation investment poorly.

### Claim 2

Innovation failure often arises in portfolio investment and surrounding governance rather than in product creation alone.

- Qualification: Individual projects can also fail through execution.
