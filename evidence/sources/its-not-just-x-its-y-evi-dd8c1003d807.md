## Curated excerpt

> *If we publicly shame people whose text looks like it might have been written by a machine – because it mimics the language used for human reasoning – and people stop writing in ways that they internalize as “AI writing” out of fear of false detection, it sends a signal that your language for reasoning must be policed, or you too could be held up to public scrutiny.*
