## Curated excerpt

> *I’m not so interested when companies make concept videos. Sure, that promotes internal and market alignment. But you’re not learning anything from at a product strategy level. I think, for really valuable thinking out loud, a company has to embark on a path that ticks every cell of a business model canvas. It’s design pathfinding but scaled up a powers-of-10 level or two.*
