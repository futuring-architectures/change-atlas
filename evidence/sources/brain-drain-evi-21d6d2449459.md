## Curated excerpt

> *Similarly to how people use the internet in healthy and unhealthy ways, I think we should expect differential effects. For skilled knowledge workers with strong confidence in their own abilities, AI will be a tool to chunk up cognitively-demanding tasks at a higher level of abstraction in accordance with Miller’s law. For others… it’ll be a crutch.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI is likely to have different cognitive effects for users with strong prior skill than for users who rely on it as a crutch.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Knowledge workers using AI for demanding tasks.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2025-02-13.
- Qualification: Confidence is not identical to competence, and novices can benefit from well-designed scaffolding.

### Claim 2

Skilled users can use AI to operate at a higher level of abstraction over cognitively demanding work.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Expert augmentation with generative systems.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2025-02-13.
- Qualification: Abstraction can conceal errors and erode lower-level knowledge if feedback is weak.
