## Curated excerpt

> *In the past, the office and corporate bureaucracies were tasked with turning idiosyncratic humans into standardized cogs. Today, the companies themselves need people to be idiosyncratic. Corporate success is no longer about throwing the highest quantity of resources into a meat grinder \[..\] In a world of non-linear output, companies need to be more flexible, to experiment with different people and projects.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Industrial-era offices and bureaucracies standardized workers, while nonlinear knowledge work increasingly depends on idiosyncratic people and contributions.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: The changing organizational requirements of knowledge-intensive firms.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2023-01-18.
- Qualification: Standardization remains useful for safety, interoperability, fairness, and repeatable service; not all contemporary work is nonlinear.

### Claim 2

Organizations facing nonlinear output should become more flexible and experiment with different combinations of people and projects.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Organization design for uncertain, creative, or specialized work.
- Date relevance: Normative position published 2023-01-18.
- Qualification: Flexibility and experimentation can increase precarity, coordination costs, duplicated work, and accountability gaps.
