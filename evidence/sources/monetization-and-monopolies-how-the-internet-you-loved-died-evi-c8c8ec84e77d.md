## Curated excerpt

> *“Tech Monopolies: They profit by creating positive externalities for society. They don’t last that long. And their rents are both reasonable and self-defeating.” Progress itself depends on the natural movements of this cycle being allowed to run unchecked, despite the unease many feel when looking at these companies in isolation \[…\] I understand the temptation to seek alternate solutions. But doing so is worse for consumers, worse for other founders, and worse for society as a whole.*
