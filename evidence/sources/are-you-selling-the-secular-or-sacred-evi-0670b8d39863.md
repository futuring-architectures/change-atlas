## Curated excerpt

> *This is a framework by which you can evaluate why users of your platform engage with it. By utilizing the tools of economics and behavioral psychology, atomic value swaps let you understand why people use your product. It strips everything down to the base level—the more confidently you can answer the question of why users engage with your core product loop, the better you’ll be able to build around it.*
