## Curated excerpt

> *The products that survive aren’t going to be the ones with the best tech or biggest teams. They’re going to be the ones where someone formed a genuine opinion about the right way to solve something and kept refining it over and over and over again. Everyone else is just shipping features into an overcrowded market and wondering why they aren’t winning.*
