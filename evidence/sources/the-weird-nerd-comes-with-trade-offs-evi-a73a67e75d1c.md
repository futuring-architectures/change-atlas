## Curated excerpt

> *Most people, while liking non-conformism in the abstract and post-facto, are not very willing to actually put up with the personality trade-offs of Weird Nerds in practice \[…\] Weird Nerds will have certain traits that might be less than ideal, that these traits come “in a package” with other, very good traits, and if one makes filtering or promotion based on the absence of those traits a priority, they will miss out on the positives.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Organizations often celebrate nonconformity abstractly while rejecting the interpersonal traits accompanying unconventional contributors.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Selection and promotion of idiosyncratic talent.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2024-06-08.
- Qualification: The package framing can excuse harmful behavior or stereotype creative people.

### Claim 2

Filtering out every undesirable trait can also remove valuable traits that are correlated within a person.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Whole-person trade-offs in talent selection.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2024-06-08.
- Qualification: Organizations should distinguish tolerable difference from conduct that harms others or undermines accountability.
