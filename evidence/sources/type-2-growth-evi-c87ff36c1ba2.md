## Curated excerpt

> *Evolutionary growth, which includes the expansion of intangibles such as freedom, wisdom, and complexity, doesn’t have similar limits. We can always figure out a way to improve things, even without using more stuff — especially without using more stuff\! There is no limit to betterment. We can keep growing (type 2\) indefinitely.*
