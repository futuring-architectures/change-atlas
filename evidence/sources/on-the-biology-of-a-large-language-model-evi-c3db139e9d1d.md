## Curated excerpt

> *The challenges we face in understanding language models resemble those faced by biologists \[…\] While the basic principles of evolution are straightforward, the biological mechanisms it produces are spectacularly intricate. Likewise, while language models are generated by simple, human-designed training algorithms, the mechanisms born of these algorithms appear to be quite complex.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Understanding language models presents challenges analogous to biology because simple generative rules can produce intricate internal mechanisms.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Mechanistic interpretability of trained language models.
- Date relevance: Source published 2025-03-27.
- Qualification: The analogy is methodological; trained models are not biological organisms and their generating processes differ.

### Claim 2

Human-designed training algorithms can produce model mechanisms complex enough to require empirical reverse engineering.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Interpretability research on Claude 3.5 Haiku.
- Date relevance: Source published 2025-03-27.
- Qualification: The paper reports partial tools and selected cases, not a complete account of the model.
