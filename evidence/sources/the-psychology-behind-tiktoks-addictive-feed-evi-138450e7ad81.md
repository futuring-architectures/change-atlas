## Curated excerpt

> *Allow users to disengage from your product with a sense of completion. If not, people will associate your product to a never-ending list of tasks (or an addictive slot machine in this case). Not only it might help your long-term user retention, but most importantly…*  
> *…it's the Humane thing to do.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Infinite feeds can leave users without a natural sense of completion and associate a product with an endless task or slot machine.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: TikTok-style infinite-feed interaction design.
- Date relevance: Case study published 2020-09-23.
- Qualification: User responses and product implementations vary, and the excerpt does not measure addiction or retention.

### Claim 2

Digital products should provide humane opportunities to disengage with a sense of completion.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Attention-intensive consumer interfaces.
- Date relevance: Position published 2020-09-23.
- Qualification: Completion cues alone do not resolve manipulative recommendation, notification, or business incentives.
