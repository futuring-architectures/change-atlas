## Curated excerpt

> *The best indicator of what people do and think is what the people around them do and think. Instead of trying to shape opinions, we need to shape networks. That’s why we need to focus our efforts on working to craft cultures rather than wordsmithing slogans. To do that, we need to understand the subtle ways we influence each other \[…\] The lesson is clear: lasting change comes less from the facts we share and more from the networks and cultures we create.*
