## Curated excerpt

> *Hierarchy is emergent, not imposed from above. This is why we see hierarchy in nature—central nervous systems, keystone species, cells, organs, and organisms—even though there is no boss of nature \[…\] Hierarchy also emerges in evolving systems, because evolution happens through composition. Complex things, like animals, are composed of simpler things, like cells, DNA, molecules, atoms. Evolution favors modules.*
