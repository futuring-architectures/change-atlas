## Curated excerpt

> *This quasi-human weirdness is why the best users of AI are often managers and teachers, people who can understand the perspective of others and correct it when it is going wrong \[…\] Rather than focusing purely on teaching people to write good prompts, we might want to spend more time teaching them to manage the AI. To getting them inside the non-existent head of the AI so that they can understand intuitively what works.*
