## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Amazon's stated automation purpose was to automate tasks and redeploy staff to new products rather than eliminate jobs.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Amazon's hands-off-the-wheel initiative as reported in 2020.
- Date relevance: Contemporaneous case published 2020-09-16.
- Qualification: The stated purpose does not establish organization-wide employment or distributional outcomes.

### Claim 2

Automation can be designed to expand organizational capacity rather than only reduce headcount.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Strategic posture toward workplace automation.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2020-09-16.
- Qualification: Redeployment requires investment, suitable work, worker mobility, and incentives that resist converting gains into cuts.
