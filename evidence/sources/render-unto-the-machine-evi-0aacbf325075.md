## Curated excerpt

> *The claim or fear that AI will displace human beings becomes plausible to the degree that we have already been complicit in a deep deskilling that has unfolded over the last few generations. Or, to put it another way, it is easier to imagine that we are replaceable when we have already outsourced many of our core human competencies \[…\] modern institutions and technologies have been schooling people toward their own future obsolescence.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI displacement appears more plausible after people have outsourced and lost practice in core human competencies over several generations.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: The relationship between prior deskilling and perceived replaceability by AI.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-07-08.
- Qualification: Perceived replaceability does not establish actual technical or economic substitutability.

### Claim 2

Modern institutions and technologies have conditioned people toward future obsolescence by progressively deskilling them.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Long-run institutional effects on human competence.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-07-08.
- Qualification: The claim is broad and does not distinguish domains where tools augment, redistribute, or create skills.
