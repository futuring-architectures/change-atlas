## Curated excerpt

> *What does slow AI look like? We’ve automatically assumed that the way we interact with it is instantaneous. Are we sure that’s right? Would it be interesting to be able to say to an AI, Look, over the next three or four months, can you give me some ideas about holidays in Greece? Do we want to make that decision immediately? I don’t think we do. I think we want to see things, refine things, consider things. I think we want to mull them over. I think we want to discuss them.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Instantaneous AI interaction is a design assumption rather than an inherent requirement.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Temporal design of human-AI interaction.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2024-09-17.
- Qualification: Many tasks genuinely benefit from fast response, while others need incubation and longitudinal context.

### Claim 2

Slow AI could revisit decisions over months, allowing people to refine, discuss, and reconsider them.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Deliberative AI services.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2024-09-17.
- Qualification: Long-lived systems introduce privacy, drift, memory, accountability, and dependency risks.
