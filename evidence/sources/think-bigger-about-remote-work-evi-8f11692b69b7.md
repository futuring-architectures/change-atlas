## Curated excerpt

> *\[R\]emote work is a general purpose technology. Like the steam engine or electrification, there will be wide impacts and spillovers through a variety of industries, and not all of them will be direct and obvious \[..\] It is easy to see why ways of working and new technologies privilege new firms who are starting from scratch, rather than existing firms who have to adapt business practices and even their pool of workers.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Remote work is a general-purpose technology whose adoption can create broad spillovers beyond the direct substitution of home for office work.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Economy-wide effects of remote-work technologies and practices.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-04-28.
- Qualification: The general-purpose-technology classification is an argument; the magnitude and distribution of spillovers require empirical testing.

### Claim 2

New firms may capture remote work's benefits more readily than incumbents that must adapt existing practices, culture, and workforces.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Comparative organizational adoption of remote work.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2022-04-28.
- Qualification: Incumbents can possess complementary assets and learning capacity, while new firms may lack scale, trust, or mature operating routines.
