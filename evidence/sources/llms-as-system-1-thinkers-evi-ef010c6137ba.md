## Curated excerpt

> *System 2 thinking is still reserved for humans. We might use LLMs to get a first draft, but we don't have the tools to do analytical thinking with LLMs (yet). Asking it to solve a complex equation will fail. Asking ChatGPT to spell ”mayonnaise” backward or count the number of letters in a complex text might fail.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Current LLMs can produce first drafts but do not provide reliable analytical, System 2 reasoning.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Capabilities of 2023-era language models on deliberate analytical tasks.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-09-24.
- Qualification: The System 1/System 2 analogy is a functional framing, not a literal cognitive diagnosis.

### Claim 2

Failures on equations, reversed spelling, and character counting expose limits hidden by fluent language output.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Simple tasks used to probe 2023-era LLM reasoning and symbol manipulation.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-09-24.
