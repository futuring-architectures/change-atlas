## Curated excerpt

> *If you are acquiring tens or hundreds of millions of users with a new kind of service, and they are attributing value and attention to you, and the users, attention and value have network effects and hence probably winner-takes-all effects, and if they come with little or no marginal cost, then the revenue can and probably should come later. It is probably more important to focus on building the value than making money from the value \- revenue is a feature…*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

A low-marginal-cost consumer service with strong network effects may rationally prioritize user, attention, and value accumulation before monetization.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: High-growth consumer internet businesses with network effects and low marginal costs.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-08-25.
- Qualification: The logic does not apply to low-margin 'tractor' businesses, and network growth does not guarantee durable market power or eventual monetization.

### Claim 2

For a genuine network-effect rocket ship, revenue can be treated as a feature to build after the value network has formed.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Sequencing growth and monetization in the specified business model.
- Date relevance: Normative position published 2022-08-25.
- Qualification: Delayed monetization depends on financing, retention, market structure, and the ability to introduce revenue without destroying participant value.
