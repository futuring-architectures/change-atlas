## Curated excerpt

> *We're missing something big to get machines to learn efficiently, like humans and animals do. We don't know what it is yet. I don't want to bash those systems or say they’re useless—I spent my career working on them. But we have to dampen the excitement some people have that we're just going to scale this up and pretty soon we’re gonna get human intelligence. Absolutely not.*
