## Curated excerpt

> *\[…\] There is a prevalent belief out there that technology is something very modern and exclusively digital. The truth is that every single idea we’ve kept and then passed down from one generation to the next—all of those ideas are a form of technology. Every beneficial idea that paved the way for even better ideas represented a stepping stone along our technological pathway.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Technology includes ideas retained and transmitted across generations, not only modern digital artifacts.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: A broad definition of technology as inherited practical and cultural knowledge.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-07-06.
- Qualification: A definition this broad can blur useful distinctions among tools, techniques, institutions, and ideas.

### Claim 2

Beneficial inherited ideas accumulate as stepping stones along a continuing technological pathway.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Cumulative cultural and technological development.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-07-06.
- Qualification: Transmission also preserves harmful ideas, loses knowledge, and follows branching rather than uniformly progressive paths.
