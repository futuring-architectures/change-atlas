## Curated excerpt

> *\[The Internet\] It doesn’t care what you create, only that you keep creating. Make more. Make faster. Make lighter. (Make \*slop\* if you have too.) Make something that can be consumed in a breath and discarded just as quickly. Heavy things take time. And here, time is a tax. And so, we oblige—everyone does. We create more than ever, but it weighs nothing.*
