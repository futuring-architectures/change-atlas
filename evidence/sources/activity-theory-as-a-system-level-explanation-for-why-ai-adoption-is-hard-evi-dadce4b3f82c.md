## Curated excerpt

> *Failed AI adoption isn’t a sign that the technology is wrong, or that the people are. It is the system surfacing tensions that were always latent in how the work was organised. The organisations that get the most from AI won’t be the ones that trained their people hardest. They’ll be the ones that read low adoption as a system needing redesign.*
