## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

There is no single correct sequence for validating and building a startup because timing, luck, grit, and case-specific conditions matter.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Early-stage startup idea selection and validation.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-05-10.

Reverse-engineering how successful entrepreneurs developed conviction can reveal recurring validation patterns without producing a universal formula.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Cross-case learning from eight startup histories in the source.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2022-05-10.
- Qualification: Retrospective selection of successful cases creates survivorship and narrative biases.
