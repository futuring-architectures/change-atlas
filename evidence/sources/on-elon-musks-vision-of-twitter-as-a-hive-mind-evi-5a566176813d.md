## Curated excerpt

> *So, Elon’s premise that Twitter can behave like a collective intelligence only holds if the structure of the network and nature of interactions is tuned to promote collective outcomes. Everything we know suggests the design space that would promote effective collective behavior at scale—if it exists—is quite small compared to the possible design space on the internet.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Large-scale social networks evaluated as systems of collective intelligence.

- Qualification: The condition does not specify which structures produce which collective outcomes or whose definition of a desirable outcome governs tuning.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Designs for collective behavior on large internet platforms.

- Qualification: The claim extrapolates from collective-behavior research and is not a measured map of the internet platform design space.
