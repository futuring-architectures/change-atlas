## Curated excerpt

> *We’re building the infrastructure of future human experience without explicitly discussing what that experience should be \[…\] The most urgent conversation about AI isn’t about its capabilities or risks, but about the kind of life we want it to help us create. Until we grapple with these deeper questions about human flourishing, our technical debates will continue to miss the point and further alienate us from one another.*
