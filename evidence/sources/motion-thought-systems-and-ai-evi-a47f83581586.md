## Curated excerpt

> *AI systems are attempting to automate thought. The parallels to the automation of motion during the industrial revolution are striking. Today, the automation is still crude: we’re doing the cognitive equivalent of pumping water and hammering—basic tasks like summarization, pattern recognition, and text generation. We haven’t yet figured out how to build robust engines for this new source of energy—we’re not even at the locomotive stage of AI yet.*
