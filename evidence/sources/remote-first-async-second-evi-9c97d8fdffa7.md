## Curated excerpt

> *Many makers work inside offices. But most offices hinder makers’ productivity. How could they not? The office as we know it is the physical embodiment of the manager’s schedule. It is a place for observing, instructing, receiving updates, and providing feedback. An office is a management tool. And offices work as intended. They empower managers. But they handicap makers.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The conventional office embodies a manager's need to observe, instruct, receive updates, and give feedback.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Office design for knowledge work.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2022-06-24.
- Qualification: Offices can also support maker collaboration, social learning, belonging, equipment access, and spontaneous discovery.

### Claim 2

Workplaces optimized for managerial visibility can handicap makers by fragmenting concentration.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Creative knowledge work requiring long periods of focus.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2022-06-24.
- Qualification: Async work can create delays, isolation, documentation burdens, and exclusion when poorly designed.
