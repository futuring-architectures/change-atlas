## Curated excerpt

> *Is what you're doing taking a large amount of text and asking the LLM to convert it into a smaller amount of text? Then it's probably going to be great at it. If you're asking it to convert into a roughly equal amount of text it will be so-so. If you're asking it to create more text than you gave it, forget about it.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

LLMs are strongest when compressing a large input into a smaller output.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Practical experience building language-model applications.
- Date relevance: Source published 2025-01-20.
- Qualification: Performance varies with model, domain, context quality, and what counts as successful compression.

### Claim 2

LLM performance degrades as requested output length approaches or exceeds input length.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Input-output expansion ratio as a heuristic for LLM application design.
- Date relevance: Source published 2025-01-20.
- Qualification: The heuristic is experiential rather than a universal scaling law and does not cover generation from latent model knowledge.
