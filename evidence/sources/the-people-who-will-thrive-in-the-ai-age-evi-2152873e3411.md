## Curated excerpt

> *I’d say that a guiding principle of the emerging AI age is this: When intelligence is plentiful, volition is valuable. The people who are going to make a difference are not the ones who seek relaxation and passively use AI to work less. They are the ones who will seek improvement and actively wrestle with AI to develop their own mental capabilities and accomplish more.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

When machine intelligence is plentiful, sustained human volition becomes comparatively scarce and valuable.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Personal agency in an AI-rich society.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2026-06-28.
- Qualification: Volition depends on material security, health, education, institutions, and social support.

### Claim 2

People who actively use AI to develop their capabilities will contribute more than those who use it mainly to reduce effort.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Individual trajectories in the emerging AI era.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2026-06-28.
- Qualification: Rest, care, and refusal can be rational; contribution cannot be reduced to output.
