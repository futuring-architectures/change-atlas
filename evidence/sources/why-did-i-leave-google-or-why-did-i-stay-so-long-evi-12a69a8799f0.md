## Curated excerpt

> *We start companies to build products that serve people, not to sit in meetings with lawyers. You need to be able to answer the "what have I done for our users today" question with "not much but I got promoted" and be happy with that answer to be successful in Corp-Tech … The innovation challenges at Corp-Tech will only get worse as the risk tolerance will go down. Soon, Lawyers \> Builders and the builders will need to go elsewhere to start new companies.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

In the author's account, promotion incentives and falling risk tolerance at Google privileged corporate process over building for users.

- Classification: `observation`
- Scope: Noam Bardin's retrospective account of working at Google after its acquisition of Waze.
- Date relevance: Original publication date recovered as 2021-02-17.
- Qualification: This is a senior participant's perspective, not a representative assessment of Google or all acquired teams.

### Claim 2

When internal advancement rewards procedural navigation more than user-facing building, successful corporations can drive builders elsewhere.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Large technology firms with mature legal, promotion, and risk systems.
- Date relevance: Interpretation published 2021-02-17.
- Qualification: Legal and review functions can protect users and firms; the relevant issue is balance and incentive design.
