## Curated excerpt

> *Two clocks are running, and they have fallen out of sync. The first measures the progress of the technology. It ticks forward every few weeks \[…\] The second tracks the institutions meant to use the technology, and it moves the way institutions always move \[…\] The distance between those clocks is the most important fact in business right now. The public argument about AI mostly misses this gap because it is almost entirely an argument about the first clock.*
