## Curated excerpt

> *Economic actors are increasingly machines and this machine economy will be larger over time…The internet isn’t dead \- it is alive with machines…Human-based coordination mechanisms aren’t relevant as machines don't use language and trust relationships the way humans do. Our current coordination layer is designed for human-to-human interaction, but now we need one that works at machine scale and speed.*

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Coordination systems for autonomous or semi-autonomous economic agents.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2025-07-18.
- Qualification: Machine-readable identity, authority, contracts, dispute resolution, and accountability still depend on human institutions and governance.
