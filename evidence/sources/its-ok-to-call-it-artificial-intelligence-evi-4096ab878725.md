## Curated excerpt

> *Where this gets actively harmful is when people start to deploy systems under the assumption that these tools really are trustworthy, intelligent systems—capable of making decisions that have a real impact on people’s lives. This is the thing we have to fight back against: we need to help people overcome their science fiction priors, understand exactly what modern AI systems are capable of, how they can be used responsibly and what their limitations are.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI deployment becomes actively harmful when decision-makers assume current systems are trustworthy and intelligent enough to make decisions with real effects on people's lives.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: High-impact deployment of contemporary AI systems.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-01-07.
- Qualification: Risk depends on the system, task, oversight, contestability, and consequences rather than the AI label alone.

Responsible AI use requires helping people replace science-fiction expectations with concrete knowledge of system capabilities, limitations, and appropriate uses.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Public and organizational literacy for AI deployment.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-01-07.
- Qualification: Literacy is necessary but may not substitute for technical controls, accountability, law, or institutional capacity.
