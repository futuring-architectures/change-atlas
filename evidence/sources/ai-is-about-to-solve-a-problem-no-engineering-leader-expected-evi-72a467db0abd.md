## Curated excerpt

> *When AI-assisted development clears the backlog faster than new ideas fill it, the constraint shifts from execution to ideation. The scarce resource is no longer developers who can build. It’s people who can figure out what to build next. Most engineering organizations are not structured for that \[…\] All of it assumes execution is expensive and slow. Remove that assumption and the entire planning apparatus is solving a problem that no longer exists.*

## Atomic claims
