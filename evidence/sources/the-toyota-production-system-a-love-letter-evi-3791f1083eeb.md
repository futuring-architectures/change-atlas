## Curated excerpt

> *Render unto the machines the things that are legible and repetitive, and unto humans the things that are illegible and novel. It is in this way that the Toyota Production System respects humans – it gives people the type of work at which they are able to generate the most value and feel the most fulfilled. This “cyborg” structure is the best of both worlds.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Organizations should assign legible, repetitive work to machines while preserving illegible and novel work for humans.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Division of labor in production and operational systems.
- Date relevance: Normative position published 2021-12-10.
- Qualification: Legibility is not fixed: automation changes it, and human oversight may still be required for repetitive work with safety or ethical consequences.

### Claim 2

A cyborg organizational structure combines machine consistency in repetition with human judgment in novelty and ambiguity.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Human-machine operating design inspired by the Toyota Production System.
- Date relevance: Concept articulated 2021-12-10.
- Qualification: The metaphor does not specify interfaces, escalation rules, accountability, or how the boundary should change over time.
