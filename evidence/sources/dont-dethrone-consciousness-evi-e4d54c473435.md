## Curated excerpt

> *If machines do ever more of our cognitive work, perhaps spiritually this abnegation is not some horror, but frees us to focus on experiencing the world, living in the world, rather than constantly mastering it. It is a hard thing to cede, unpleasant for our generation, but eventually—if not now, then in a decade, or a century—we will have to cede away much of our cognitive mastery. In this, perhaps the rise of AI could be framed as a return to childhood. And who are more blessed than the children?*
