## Curated excerpt

> *Many of the web’s most successful sites are those that leverage scale to their advantage, either by multiplying social connections or product choice, or by sorting the huge conglomeration of information that constitutes the internet itself. But this scale relies on masses of humans to create the underlying value, and humans can’t beat AI when it comes to mass production \[…\] Machine scale beats human curation. And the same might be true of the web.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Successful web platforms derive scale advantages from masses of humans who create the underlying social, commercial, or informational value.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Large web platforms built on user contribution and information aggregation.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-06-26.
- Qualification: The claim groups distinct platform models and does not quantify the human contribution.

### Claim 2

Machine-generated volume can overpower human curation and thereby destabilize the web’s existing scale model.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Effects of mass generative-AI production on web information ecosystems.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2023-06-26.
- Qualification: Machine volume does not necessarily win on trust, relevance, provenance, or distribution.
