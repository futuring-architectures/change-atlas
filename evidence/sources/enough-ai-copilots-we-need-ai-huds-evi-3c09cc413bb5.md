## Curated excerpt

> *Anyone serious about designing for AI should consider non-copilot form factors that more directly extend the human mind…Routine predictable work might make sense to delegate to a virtual copilot / assistant. But when you’re shooting for extraordinary outcomes, perhaps the best bet is to equip human experts with new superpowers.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

AI interfaces should explore forms that extend human cognition directly rather than defaulting to copilot agents.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Design of AI interfaces for expert work.
