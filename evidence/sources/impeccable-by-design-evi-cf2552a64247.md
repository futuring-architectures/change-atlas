## Curated excerpt

> *Mindlessly shipping more stuff faster is not a strategy, and plenty of companies are about to learn this the expensive way. The creative AI tools we build can’t just focus on lowering the barrier of entry and raising the output floor, they must also enable creatives to insert themselves into the agentic loop for the last mile. That last 20% that pushes the work out-of-distribution, out of mediocrity, beyond the average.*
