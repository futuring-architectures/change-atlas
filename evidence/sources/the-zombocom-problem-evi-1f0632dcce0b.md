## Curated excerpt

> *How do we build software that can be anything without falling into the Zombocom trap? \[…\] Doing the hard work of systems-thinking up front allows you to uncover exponential opportunities. Working back to a first best customer sharpens your edge-of-the-wedge. It can be anything, but first it has to be something specific.*
