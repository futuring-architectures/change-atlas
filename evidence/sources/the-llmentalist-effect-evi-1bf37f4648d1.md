## Curated excerpt

> *LLMs look less like an information technology and more like a modern mechanisation of the psychic hotline. Delegating your decision-making, ranking, assessment, strategising, analysis, or any other form of reasoning to a chatbot becomes the functional equivalent to phoning a psychic for advice. Imagine Google or a major tech company trying to fix their search engine by adding a psychic hotline to their front page? That’s what they’re doing with Bard.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Chat-based large language models can be understood as a mechanized version of a psychic hotline rather than as an information technology that reasons.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: A critical model of LLM interaction in July 2023.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-07-04.
- Qualification: The analogy emphasizes subjective validation and statistical plausibility but may understate useful information-processing functions.

### Claim 2

Delegating consequential decision-making, ranking, assessment, strategy, or analysis to a chatbot is functionally comparable to seeking advice from a psychic.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Use of chatbots for consequential reasoning tasks.
- Date relevance: Source published 2023-07-04.
- Qualification: The absolute comparison does not distinguish low-risk assistance, verified outputs, tool use, or systems designed with domain controls.
