## Curated excerpt

> *Cognitive debt is not an AI problem. It is a human problem that AI amplifies and, if used deliberately, can also help solve. The teams that navigate this well will not be the ones that slow down AI adoption or demand that every developer fully understand every generated line. They will be the ones that build the habit of feeding context in — and trust better tools to carry it forward.*
