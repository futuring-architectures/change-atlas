## Curated excerpt

> *LLMs in no sense “think” like people do \[…\] I don’t think this point is controversial, but it seems to often be ignored in practice, not least when people start talking about LLMs being “human-like” or indeed “superhuman”. We need to stop comparing LLMs to humans, and instead understand and evaluate what they can and cannot do, without assuming that their abilities in any way correlate with human abilities.*
