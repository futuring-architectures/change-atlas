## Curated excerpt

> *What if, instead of thinking of the metaverse as a set of interconnected virtual places, we think of it as a communications medium? \[..\] The interactions are not place based but happening in the ether between two or more connected people. The occasion is more the point than the place \[..\] This is the true internet—the network of networks, with dynamic interconnections. If the metaverse is to inherit that mantle, it has to have that same quality. Connection.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

The metaverse can be understood more productively as a communications medium than as a set of virtual places.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Definitions and architectures of the prospective metaverse.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-08-02.
- Qualification: This is a reframing, not a demonstrated exclusion of place-based experiences from metaverse systems.

### Claim 2

To inherit the internet’s role, a metaverse should preserve dynamic connection across a network of networks.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Prospective metaverse architectures claiming continuity with the open internet.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-08-02.
- Qualification: The principle does not specify the standards, governance, identity, or economic arrangements needed to realize interoperable connection.
