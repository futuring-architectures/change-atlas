## Curated excerpt

> *We seem to have accepted the job of the curator as providing a product review, a list of links, or a song recommendation — all inside linear structures and chronological feeds designed to surface the ideas of the last 24 hours, not to accumulate and surface knowledge as needed \[..\] People should be able to find whatever content they want on their terms and not be beholden to when the curator decides to publish.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Chronological feeds are designed to surface recent output rather than accumulate and retrieve knowledge when it is needed.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Curated content delivered through linear, chronological online feeds.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-05-02.
- Qualification: Some feeds combine chronological and retrieval functions; the excerpt describes their dominant orientation rather than a universal limitation.

### Claim 2

People should be able to search curated content on demand instead of depending on when a curator publishes it.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Interfaces for curated knowledge, recommendations, and content discovery.
- Date relevance: Source published 2022-05-02.
- Qualification: The principle does not determine how curation, indexing, ranking, maintenance, or funding should be implemented.
