## Curated excerpt

> *\[P\]ost-industrial society isn’t our name for the next stage of civilizational progress. Instead, the term is true in its most literal and pessimistic interpretation: a society after and without industrial civilization . . . We have lost the implicit knowledge upon which our industrial systems functioned even as recently as a few decades ago. That knowledge cannot be regained absent the people who actually built and understood those systems.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Post-industrial society may describe the loss of industrial civilization rather than a later stage of civilizational progress.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: The condition of industrial capacity in advanced societies around 2021.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-03-24.
- Qualification: The excerpt advances a deliberately pessimistic reading and does not establish how broadly industrial capability has declined.

### Claim 2

Tacit industrial knowledge cannot be recovered without people who have directly built and understood the relevant systems.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Preservation and recovery of complex industrial capability.
- Date relevance: Source published 2021-03-24.
- Qualification: Documentation, simulation, reverse engineering, and adjacent expertise may preserve or reconstruct some knowledge, so the absolute wording requires qualification.
