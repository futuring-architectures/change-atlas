## Curated excerpt

> *We can pine for the much less chaotic world of old, where a small number of gatekeepers channeled their observations about the world into their “professionally curated” packages. Or, we can choose to subject ourselves to the new endless algorithmic flow of the feed. One channels the thoughts of the few. The other, the engagement data of the masses. Both are easy to get lost in and, incomplete.*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

Legacy professional media channels the observations of a small set of gatekeepers, while algorithmic feeds channel the engagement data of large populations.

- Classification: `conceptual_claim`
- Scope: Two selection systems for mediated information.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-03-24.
- Qualification: Professional media also responds to audiences, and feeds are shaped by platform objectives and designers rather than mass data alone.

### Claim 2

Both professionally curated packages and endless algorithmic feeds are incomplete representations in which audiences can become lost.

- Classification: `interpretation`
- Scope: Limits of legacy and feed-based media environments.
- Date relevance: Source published 2024-03-24.
- Qualification: The two systems have different error modes, accountability structures, and degrees of user control.
