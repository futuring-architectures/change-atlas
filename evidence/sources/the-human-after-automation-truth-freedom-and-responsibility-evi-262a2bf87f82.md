## Curated excerpt

> *He will keep his job. He will draw a salary. He will even call himself an “expert.” But he will be a diminished version of himself, because: He will replace thought with production. He will replace the question with a prompt. He will replace judgment with approval of output. He will replace wisdom with a workflow. He will replace responsibility with “that’s how it came out of the system.”*

## Atomic claims

### Claim 1

A worker can retain title and salary while becoming diminished by replacing thought, questions, judgment, wisdom, and responsibility with workflow approval.

- Classification: `prediction`
- Scope: Human roles in heavily automated knowledge work.
- Date relevance: Forecast published 2026-06-09.
- Qualification: Automation can also expand judgment and agency depending on role design, skill, governance, and incentives.

### Claim 2

Human expertise requires responsibility for judgment, not merely approval of system output.

- Classification: `normative_position`
- Scope: Professional identity after automation.
- Date relevance: Position published 2026-06-09.
- Qualification: Responsibility must be matched with authority, information, competence, time, and institutional support.
